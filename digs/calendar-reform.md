# DIG SITE — Calendar Reform

**The question:** what do the primary documents show about how, when, and by whose authority the Western calendar was changed — and what dating discrepancies result from the staggered adoption?

## Why this trench is open
Calendar reform documents are printed statute and papal decree — the hardest Tier-1 material. The staggered adoption (1582 Catholic, 1752 British, 1918 Russia, 1923 Greece) creates dating ambiguities that affect all historical chronology in between.

## Method notes specific to this dig
- The primary documents (Inter gravissimas, British Calendar Act) are the evidence. Commentary on their intent is Tier 3.
- The mathematical rationale (correcting Julian drift) is documented and not contested. The question is whether ONLY drift was corrected, or whether additional time manipulation occurred.
- Phantom time hypothesis (Illig, 1991) is SPECULATIVE and must be tagged as such if it appears.
