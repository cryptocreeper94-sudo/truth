# DIG SITE — Map Chronology

**The question:** what does the dated sequence of surviving maps actually show about how the depicted world changed — and when, and in whose hands?

## Why this trench is open first
The book (*Through The Veil*, ch. 36C "The Relocated Map") argues from map chronology; the source trail is personally known; and maps are the strongest Tier-1 material there is — mass-printed, widely scattered, dated, and comparable state-by-state.

## Method notes specific to this dig
- Map claims live and die on **states and editions**. Every source record must say which state/plate/edition it describes.
- CHRONOLOGY links are the workhorse here: what a feature looked like across dated editions. TEXTUAL-MATCH applies to cartouches and annotations.
- The temptation in this territory is reading meaning off a single map. One map is an anecdote; a dated sequence is evidence.
