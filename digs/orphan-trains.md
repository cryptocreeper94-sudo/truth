# DIG SITE — Orphan Trains

**The question:** what do the institutional and survivor records actually establish about the scale, mechanics, and consent conditions of the orphan train movement (1854–1929)?

## Why this trench is open
The Children's Aid Society's own records are the primary source. Survivor testimony adds a second layer. Both are available and neither party disputes the program's existence.

## Method notes specific to this dig
- Institutional records (annual reports, placement records) are Tier 1. Survivor testimony is Tier 4 (valuable, tagged as what it is).
- The scale estimate (250,000) comes from the institution's own reporting. Independent verification of individual cases is possible through census records and land records.
- The question of consent is the contested territory: the institution's records describe the program as rescue; survivor testimony sometimes describes it as kidnapping. Both accounts must be recorded.
