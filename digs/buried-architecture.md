# DIG SITE — Buried Architecture

**The question:** what do surviving buildings, underground districts, and municipal records show about cities whose original ground floors now sit below current street grade?

## Why this trench is open
The buildings are physical objects. They can be visited. The buried floors are visible. Municipal records document street regrading in many cases. This is Tier-1 territory.

## Method notes specific to this dig
- Municipal engineering records are the key companion to the physical evidence. Where regrading is documented (Seattle, Portland), the explanation is strong.
- The "mud flood" hypothesis posits a single catastrophic event. A CHRONOLOGY link showing simultaneous burial across geographically separated cities would be significant. Without that link, individual explanations (fire regrading, normal accumulation, infrastructure) hold.
- Photographs and architectural details are powerful but must be dated and located precisely.
