# DIG SITE — Monetary System

**The question:** what do the foundational statutes and institutional records show about how the U.S. monetary system was structured, restructured, and by whose authority?

## Why this trench is open
The statutes are printed law. The institutions exist. Their own descriptions of themselves are on the record. This is pure Tier-1 territory.

## Method notes specific to this dig
- Statutes are the primary evidence. Institutional self-descriptions (FAQs, reports) are secondary but still Tier 1 (what an institution says about itself is a primary source for its own structure).
- The Jekyll Island meeting (1910) is documented in participants' own later accounts but is Tier 2 (contemporary memoir). It does not become Tier 1 without independent institutional records.
- "Debt-based economy" is an interpretive frame. The mechanics of fractional reserve banking are documented; their evaluation is a separate claim.
