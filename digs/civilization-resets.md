# DIG SITE — Civilization Resets

**The question:** what does the physical, geological, and documentary record show about catastrophic discontinuities in the built environment and written record — events after which the prior state of civilization appears to reset, degrade, or vanish?

## Why this trench is open
This is the meta-dig. The buried architecture dig, the map chronology dig, the orphan trains dig, and the giant skeleton reports dig all touch different edges of the same question: is there evidence of historical continuity being *broken*, and if so, when, how many times, and by what mechanism? This dig catalogs the pattern across sub-digs.

## Method notes specific to this dig
- **This is the dig most vulnerable to confirmation bias. Every rule in METHOD.md applies with extra force here.**
- "Reset" is the hypothesis. The evidence is physical anomalies, documentary gaps, and architectural discontinuities. Each piece of evidence has its own explanation that does not require a "reset." The archive's job is to catalog both the anomalies AND the explanations, then let the reader see whether a pattern emerges from the *documented* links.
- Tier-1-or-silence applies to every sub-topic:
  - **Meltology / liquefied architecture:** Structures showing apparent thermal alteration, vitrified forts, "melted" buildings. The physical objects exist; the interpretation is contested.
  - **Mud flood / buried cities:** Documented in the buried-architecture dig. Municipal records explain most cases. The question is whether a CHRONOLOGY link connects cases across geographies.
  - **Orphan generation:** The orphan trains and foundling hospitals of the 19th century moved hundreds of thousands of children whose origins were not recorded. The institutional records exist.
  - **Reset of knowledge:** Technologies present in ancient structures (acoustic engineering, precision stonework) that were not replicated for centuries. The structures exist; the dating and interpretation are contested.
- SPECULATIVE claims are allowed to exist but NEVER count as corroboration. Two speculative links do not equal one documented one. A thousand don't.

## Sub-Topics

### Vitrified Forts
Approximately 70 vitrified forts have been documented across Scotland, France, and Scandinavia — stone structures where the walls have been fused by extreme heat into a glass-like mass. The physical objects are Tier 1. The cause (deliberate destruction? siege technique? unknown energy event?) is CONTESTED.

### Liquefied/Melted Architecture
Buildings, particularly in older European and Asian cities, showing stone and brick deformation patterns consistent with extreme heat exposure. Photographs document the current state; interpretation of cause is SPECULATIVE without dated geological analysis.

### Documentary Gaps
Periods where the written record thins dramatically or contradicts physical evidence. The "Dark Ages" gap in Western European documentation is the most studied example. Illig's phantom time hypothesis (1991) proposes 297 fabricated years — tagged SPECULATIVE, never corroborating.

### Orphan Generation
Linked to the orphan trains dig. The question: where did hundreds of thousands of "orphaned" children come from simultaneously? The institutional records document the scale; the origin question is CONTESTED.

### Technological Discontinuity
Ancient structures demonstrating engineering capabilities that were not replicated for centuries (Great Pyramid, Roman concrete, Antikythera mechanism). Each case is individually DOCUMENTED. Whether they form a pattern of reset requires CHRONOLOGY links with corpus statements.

## Counter-exhibits
*(Required with maximum force. (1) Each alleged "anomaly" has an individual, local explanation that does not require a global reset — fire regrading, normal urban accumulation, population displacement by documented wars/famines/epidemics, gradual loss of specific craft techniques through economic or political disruption. (2) The "Dark Ages" were not actually dark — significant documentation survives from the Byzantine Empire, the Islamic Golden Age, and monasteries; the gap is primarily in Western European secular records. (3) The human tendency to see patterns in unrelated events is extensively documented in psychology. (4) "Reset" theories require extraordinary evidence because they claim a worldwide event that left no surviving documentary record from any literate civilization — an absence that itself requires explanation.)*
