# DIG SITE — Institutional Capture

**The question:** what do official records show about documented cases where government agencies or state-adjacent institutions altered records, concealed programs, or operated without the oversight their charters promised?

## Why this trench is open
This dig is different from the others: the evidence here is *mainstream acknowledged history*. The claims are not contested — they are confirmed by the institutions themselves. The purpose of this trench is to document the *pattern* of institutional behavior using the archive's own discipline, so that claims about *undocumented* institutional behavior (elsewhere in the archive) can be evaluated against a baseline of what institutions have *admittedly* done.

## Method notes specific to this dig
- This is the archive's strongest territory. The sources are government records, declassified files, congressional testimony, and statutes. Everything here is Tier 1.
- The temptation is to extrapolate: "they did THIS, therefore they probably did THAT." That extrapolation is legitimate as a hypothesis but must be its own card tagged SPECULATIVE. Documented cases do not automatically validate undocumented claims.
- Counter-exhibits here would include cases where institutional oversight *worked* — where misconduct was caught, prosecuted, and corrected.
