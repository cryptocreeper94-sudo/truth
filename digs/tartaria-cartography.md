# DIG SITE — Tartaria Cartography

**The question:** what does the dated sequence of European maps and encyclopedias show about how "Tartary" was depicted, described, and eventually dropped from the cartographic and reference record?

## Why this trench is open
Maps are Tier-1 material — mass-printed, widely scattered, dated, and comparable state-by-state. The appearance and disappearance of a geographic term across three centuries of independent cartographers is a pattern that can be documented without interpretation.

## Method notes specific to this dig
- CHRONOLOGY links are the primary tool: tracking the term across dated editions.
- The question is WHAT the maps show, not WHAT Tartary was. Whether it was a civilization, a label, or a placeholder is interpretation for claim cards, not source records.
- The Britannica edition comparison (1771 → modern) provides a parallel textual track.
