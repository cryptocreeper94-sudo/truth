# DIG SITE — New Madrid, 1811–1812

**The question:** what does the surviving physical and administrative record actually establish about the scale and nature of the New Madrid events — as distinct from both the modern textbook account and any interpretive frame?

## Why this trench is open
The book (*Through The Veil*, ch. 36C "The Great River") argues an interpretive frame over these events. The archive's job here is stricter and prior: pin down what the hardest record shows, at full discipline, so that any interpretation — the book's included — can be tested against it rather than against anecdote.

## Method notes specific to this dig
- The legal/administrative layer (statutes, claims files, land patents) is the strongest material and opens first.
- The famous narrative details (river running backward, the comet, Tecumseh) are Tier-2 contemporary-record work — newspapers of the week, dated diaries — and wait until Tier 2 opens. Do not import them into Tier-1 cards.
- The certificate-speculation fraud is part of the record, not an embarrassment to hide: it must appear in every card that leans on individual claim files.
