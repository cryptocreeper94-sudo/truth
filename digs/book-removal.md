# DIG SITE — Book Removal

**The question:** which books were present in the 1611 King James Bible that are absent from modern editions, and what is the documented history of their removal?

## Why this trench is open
The 1611 and modern KJV are both physical objects that can be directly compared. The difference in their tables of contents is a textual fact, not an interpretation.

## Method notes specific to this dig
- The comparison is between two dated, physical editions of the same work. This is as close to a controlled experiment as textual history gets.
- The theological arguments for and against inclusion are Tier 3/4 material — institutional positions and tradition. They belong in the objection section of claim cards, not as evidence.
- The British and Foreign Bible Society's 1826 decision is the key institutional pivot point.
