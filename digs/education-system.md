# DIG SITE — Education System

**The question:** what do the foundational documents and institutional records show about who designed the modern American public education system, what they stated their goals to be, and how those goals map to the system's actual structure?

## Why this trench is open
The founding documents are published, attributed, and held in institutional archives. The stated intentions of the system's architects are in their own words.

## Method notes specific to this dig
- Published documents by named authors (Gates, Rockefeller, Carnegie, Dewey, Wundt) are Tier 1 when held in institutional archives.
- The distinction between STATED intent and ACTUAL intent is critical. This dig documents what the founders SAID. Whether they meant something else is a separate claim.
- The Prussian model connection (American educators studying in Prussia in the 1840s) is documented in their own published accounts.
