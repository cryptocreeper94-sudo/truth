# C-0116 — World Fair Construction Timeline Photos vs. Claimed Timelines

## The claim
Photographic records from the construction of the 1893 World's Columbian Exposition in Chicago show significant discrepancies between the documented timeline of construction and the claimed duration of 2-3 years for completion. Specific photos indicate rapid progress that suggests a much shorter timeline than officially stated.

## The evidence
1. **Photographic documentation**: Photos from the Library of Congress, catalog number LC-DIG-PPMSCA-03276, show various stages of construction dated between 1892 and 1893.
2. **Newspaper records**: Articles from the *Chicago Tribune* detail construction progress and timelines, notably the article dated June 15, 1892, highlighting rapid advancements in construction.

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that the photographs may not accurately represent the entire construction process and could depict only selected moments that give the impression of expedited construction. Additionally, the time taken for finishing touches and landscaping is often not represented in such photographs, which could account for discrepancies in timelines.
