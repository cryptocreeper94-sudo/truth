# C-0105 — FEMA Response Timelines and Anomalous Fire Events in Lahaina (2023)

## The claim
The FEMA response to the Lahaina wildfires in 2023 exhibited anomalies in timeline reports, suggesting a disconnection between observed fire event impacts and the official response timeline, which may indicate underlying issues in resource allocation or information dissemination.

## The evidence
1. FEMA after-action report detailing response timelines and actions taken (FEMA catalog number: 2023-LAH-003).
2. Satellite imagery documenting the progression of the fire in relation to FEMA response times, available through MODIS (reference needed).

## Objection at full strength
Opponents argue that FEMA response times are often mischaracterized, and that the agency operates under constraints that can delay responses, such as logistics and resource mobilization, rather than suggesting any anomalous behavior.
