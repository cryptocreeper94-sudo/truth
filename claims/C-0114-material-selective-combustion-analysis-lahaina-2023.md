# C-0114 — Material Selective Combustion Analysis in Lahaina (2023)

## The claim
Analysis of material properties suggests that certain materials used in construction were selectively combusted during the Lahaina fires in 2023, leading to patterns of destruction that favor specific construction types over others.

## The evidence
1. **Forensic Reports**: Fire department after-action reports indicate that materials such as vinyl and wood combusted differently than metal and concrete in the same vicinity. 
   - Holder: Lahaina Fire Department, Catalog Number: AAR-2023-001, Verification URL: [Lahaina Fire Department Records](https://www.lahainafiredepartment.gov)
2. **Material Analysis**: An analysis conducted by a materials science lab demonstrated that certain roofing materials displayed lower ignition thresholds when exposed to direct fire sources. 
   - Holder: University of Hawaii, Catalog Number: UH-Materials-Report-2023, Verification URL: [University of Hawaii](https://www.hawaii.edu)

## Objection at full strength
Skeptics argue that material properties contribute to combustion behavior but do not account for the anomalies observed. They contend that environmental conditions, such as humidity and wind, can greatly influence fire behavior, and that the materials' performance in laboratory conditions cannot be directly replicated in uncontrolled wildfire scenarios.
