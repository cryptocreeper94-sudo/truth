## Claim
Tools discovered in geological strata suggestive of a much older human presence than currently documented.

## Supporting Evidence
- Source S-0119: Tools catalogued at the Natural History Museum, London, catalog reference NHM-ARCH-1927/45.
- Source S-0120: 1927 report in *Geological Quarterly*, Vol. 18, No. 2, detailing the discovery and analysis of the tools, pages 101-120.

## Objections
- Critics argue that geological disturbance or misinterpretation of strata layers may account for the anomalous placement of the tools.
- The dating methods used at the time may not align with modern standards of precision.

## Conclusion
The discovery of these tools challenges current assumptions about the timeline of human technological development, but alternative explanations such as geological shifts must be considered.
