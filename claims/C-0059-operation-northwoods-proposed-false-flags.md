Operation Northwoods was a proposed plan by the U.S. Department of Defense to stage false-flag terrorist attacks on American soil as a pretext for military intervention in Cuba.

## Sources

1. **Joint Chiefs of Staff Memorandum**
   - Institution: National Archives and Records Administration (NARA)
   - Record Group: RG 218
   - Series: Records of the U.S. Joint Chiefs of Staff
   - Reference: https://www.archives.gov/files/research/jfk/releases/docid-32110687.pdf

2. **National Security Archive**
   - Institution: The George Washington University
   - Document Title: "Pentagon Proposed Pretexts for Cuba Invasion in 1962"
   - Reference: https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu/news/20010430/northwoods.pdf

## Objections

- The plan was never approved or implemented, leading some to argue that its significance is overstated and merely reflects internal deliberations rather than concrete intentions.
- Skeptics point out that no direct evidence links the proposal to subsequent U.S. actions against Cuba, suggesting it was an isolated military idea rather than a broader policy.
