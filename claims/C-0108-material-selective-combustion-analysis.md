# C-0108 — Material-selective combustion analysis in Paradise (2018)

## The claim
The 2018 Paradise fire displayed evidence of material-selective combustion, where certain materials, such as metal and tile, showed resilience against the flames compared to organic materials, resulting in anomalous survival rates among structures.

## The evidence
1. Satellite imagery analysis from MODIS showing differential burn patterns in Paradise; catalog number: PAR2018-MODIS-002, held by NASA.
2. Fire department after-action reports indicating the types of materials that survived and their locations, catalog number: PF-2018-AAR-001, held by the Paradise Fire Department.

## Objection at full strength
Opponents of the material-selective combustion theory point to the variability of fire behavior influenced by topography and wind conditions, which can create pockets of intense heat that affect material differently, making the claims of selective burning less compelling.
