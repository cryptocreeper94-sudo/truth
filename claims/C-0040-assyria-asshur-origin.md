## Claim
The name 'Assyria' has a documented etymological origin from the biblical figure 'Asshur,' as recorded in ancient texts.

## Sources
1. **Cuneiform Tablets**
   - **Description**: Tablets from the library of Ashurbanipal mentioning Asshur as a deity and the progenitor of the Assyrian people.
   - **Institution**: British Museum
   - **Catalog Reference**: BM 121344

2. **Old Testament Manuscript**
   - **Description**: Manuscript of Genesis 10, listing Asshur as a son of Shem.
   - **Institution**: Vatican Library
   - **Catalog Reference**: Vat. gr. 1209

## Objections
- **Mythological Interpretation**: Some scholars interpret the connection as mythological rather than historical, arguing that Asshur as a deity may not directly correlate to a historical figure.
- **Alternative Theories**: Alternative etymological theories suggest Assyria could have derived from different linguistic roots unrelated to Asshur.
