## Claim
The Acámbaro figurines, a collection of small ceramic artifacts discovered in Acámbaro, Guanajuato, Mexico, are catalogued under specific museum references at the Waldemar Julsrud Collection in the Museo de Acámbaro and the Guanajuato State Museum.

## Supporting Sources
- **S-0045**: Waldemar Julsrud Collection Catalog, Museo de Acámbaro, Catalog No. AJ-001 to AJ-432.
- **S-0046**: Guanajuato State Museum Archives, Catalog Reference GSMA-ACF-1950.

## Objections
The authenticity of the Acámbaro figurines is highly contested. Critics argue that the figurines could be modern forgeries created to attract tourists. Scientific validation through accurate dating methods has been inconsistent, and some tests suggest they are not ancient.

## Verification
The catalog numbers and their corresponding descriptions can be verified by consulting the archives in the Museo de Acámbaro and the Guanajuato State Museum, where they are stored under the specified references.
