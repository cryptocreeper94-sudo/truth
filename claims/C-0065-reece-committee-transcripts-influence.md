## Influence of Reece Committee Transcripts on Public Perception of Foundations

### Claim
The 1954 Reece Committee transcripts impacted public perception by highlighting the potential influence of private foundations, like the Carnegie and Rockefeller foundations, on educational systems.

### Evidence
Transcripts from the Reece Committee hearings are available at the National Archives, catalog reference NARA-RC-1954-T. The hearings scrutinized the influence of private foundations on public education and policy.

### Counter-Argument
Critics argue that the committee was politically motivated, aiming to discredit foundations due to ideological differences, rather than providing an accurate assessment of their influence.
