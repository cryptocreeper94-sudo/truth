## Claim
The genealogies of Japheth as outlined in Genesis 10 and 1 Chronicles 1 are associated with various ancient European tribes, including Gomer, Magog, Madai, Javan, Tubal, Meshech, and Tiras.

## Sources
- **Genesis 10** in the Hebrew Bible, specifying the descendants of Japheth and their territories (S-0134).
- **Book of Jubilees 8-9**, which provides additional context and territorial associations for Japheth's descendants (S-0135).

## Objections
The identification of Japheth's descendants with historical European tribes is controversial. Some scholars argue that there is insufficient archaeological evidence to support these claims, suggesting instead that these genealogies reflect a mythological framework rather than historical reality.
