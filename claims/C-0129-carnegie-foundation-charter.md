# C-0129 — Carnegie Foundation Charter Founding Details

## The claim
The Carnegie Foundation for the Advancement of Teaching was established in 1905 through its charter, which aimed to promote the advancement of education and the welfare of teachers in the United States. The foundation was created with a significant endowment from Andrew Carnegie.

## The evidence
- **Source**: Carnegie Foundation Charter Document, 1905 
- **Holder**: Carnegie Foundation Archives, Catalog Number CF-001 
- **Verify at**: https://www.carnegiefoundation.org 

## Objection at full strength
There are criticisms regarding the influence of Andrew Carnegie's wealth on educational policies and practices, with some arguing that the foundation's initiatives reflect Carnegie's personal beliefs and agenda rather than a neutral pursuit of educational improvement.
