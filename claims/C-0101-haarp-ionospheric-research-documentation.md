# C-0101 — HAARP Ionospheric Research Instrument Documentation

## The claim
The High-Frequency Active Auroral Research Program (HAARP) has been involved in ionospheric research that includes experiments aimed at modifying the ionosphere for various scientific purposes, including climate studies and communication improvements.

## The evidence
1. **HAARP Research Documentation** - Various technical papers and reports on HAARP's objectives and operations.
   - Holder: U.S. Air Force Research Laboratory
   - Catalog Reference: HAARP Technical Documents
   - Verification URL: https://www.haarp.alaska.edu/

## Objection at full strength
Skeptics claim that the HAARP program is often mischaracterized, and its scientific objectives are sometimes conflated with unfounded conspiracy theories regarding weather manipulation. The lack of conclusive evidence proving that HAARP has successfully altered weather patterns raises significant doubt regarding its operational impact.
