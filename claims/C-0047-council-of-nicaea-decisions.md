The Council of Nicaea in 325 AD addressed several key issues within the early Christian Church, yet popular claims often misrepresent its decisions. The Council primarily focused on the Arian controversy, establishing the Nicene Creed to affirm the divinity of Christ.

**Actual Decisions:**
- Condemnation of Arianism.
- Establishment of the Nicene Creed.
- Fixing the date of Easter.

**Popular Claims:**
- That the Council decided the canon of the Bible. This is not supported by contemporary records and is a later misconception.

**Source Citations:**

The primary records illustrate that the Council's focus was doctrinal, not scriptural canonization.
