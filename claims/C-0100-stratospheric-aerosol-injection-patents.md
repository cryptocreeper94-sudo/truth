# C-0100 — Stratospheric Aerosol Injection Patents

## The claim
There are several patents filed for technologies related to stratospheric aerosol injection (SAI), specifically aiming to mitigate climate change effects through solar radiation management. Notable patents include the USPTO number 8,877,726 for methods of reflecting solar radiation.

## The evidence
1. **U.S. Patent No. 8,877,726** - Methods for reflecting solar radiation to reduce global warming. 
   - Holder: U.S. Patent and Trademark Office
   - Catalog Reference: 8,877,726
   - Verification URL: https://patents.google.com/patent/US8877726B2/en
2. **U.S. Patent No. 9,190,800** - Methods for enhancing atmospheric conditions through aerosol dispersal.
   - Holder: U.S. Patent and Trademark Office
   - Catalog Reference: 9,190,800
   - Verification URL: https://patents.google.com/patent/US9190800B2/en

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that patents do not imply the existence of operational technologies, and many of these patents may represent theoretical ideas rather than practical applications. Additionally, the environmental impacts of such geoengineering methods remain contentious and largely untested at scale, leading to ethical concerns about their implementation.
