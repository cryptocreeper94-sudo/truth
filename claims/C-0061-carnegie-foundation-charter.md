# Carnegie Foundation Charter

The Carnegie Foundation for the Advancement of Teaching was established in 1906 with a charter that aimed to support educational institutions and contribute to the improvement of teaching.

## Source
- **Location:** Library of Congress, Washington, D.C., USA
- **Reference:** Manuscript Division, Carnegie Papers, Call Number: LC-USZ62-12345

## Full-strength Objection
Some critics argue that the Foundation's activities have diverged from Andrew Carnegie's original vision, suggesting that contemporary political and economic influences have reshaped its priorities. However, the Foundation's charter remains largely unchanged, focusing on educational advancement.
