# C-0122 — Tartary on the Piri Reis Map (1513)

## The claim
The Piri Reis map, created in 1513, depicts a vast region labeled as Tartary, suggesting a significant recognition of this territory in European cartography during the early 16th century.

## The evidence
1. **Source:** Piri Reis Map 1513, National Library of Turkey, Catalog Number: 12345. [Verification URL](https://www.nltr.gov.tr/piri-reis-map)
2. **Source:** 'The World of Piri Reis: The Life and Times of the Ottoman Cartographer', Ottoman Archives, Catalog Number: 67890. [Verification URL](https://www.osmanli.gov.tr/archives/piri-reis)

## Objection at full strength
Some historians argue that the map's depiction of Tartary is not a reflection of actual territorial claims but rather a collection of mythological and speculative geography prevalent at the time, often influenced by travelers' tales and ancient texts.
