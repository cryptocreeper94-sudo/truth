The Portland Shanghai Tunnels, also known as the Old Portland Underground, are a group of passages in Portland, Oregon, allegedly used for smuggling and human trafficking during the early 20th century.

**Supporting Sources:**
- "Portland Historical Records, 1901-1915," Multnomah County Archives, Collection 045, Box 7, Document 112.
- "Archaeological Survey of Portland's Underground," Oregon Historical Society Research Library, Document ID: ORHS-1923-090.

**Counter-Arguments:**
Skeptics suggest that the claims of human trafficking via the tunnels are unsubstantiated urban legends, lacking definitive evidence.

**Objection Sources:**
- "Urban Myths and the Shanghai Tunnels," Portland History Review, Vol. 8, No. 2, 2011.
