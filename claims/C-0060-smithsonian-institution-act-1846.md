# Smithsonian Institution Act (1846)

The Smithsonian Institution was established by an Act of Congress in 1846. The Act provided for the creation of an establishment for the "increase and diffusion of knowledge." The funding came from a bequest by James Smithson, a British scientist.

## Source
- **Location:** National Archives, Washington, D.C., USA
- **Reference:** Record Group 46, Records of the U.S. Senate, National Archives Identifier: 306397

## Full-strength Objection
Critics argue that the Smithsonian Institution Act's language is vague, leading to various interpretations about its primary mission. Some historians suggest the Smithsonian was intended to function more as a research institution rather than a public museum. This interpretation is contested by those who emphasize its public educational role.
