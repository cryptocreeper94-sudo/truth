## Claim
The descendants of Ham, as listed in Genesis 10, are linked to regions and peoples in Africa and the Near East, including Cush, Mizraim, Phut, and Canaan.

## Sources
- **Genesis 10** in the Hebrew Bible, presenting the genealogy and territorial claims of Ham's descendants (S-0132).
- **Josephus Antiquities I.6**, which elaborates on the territories occupied by Ham's lineage (S-0133).

## Objections
The connection between Ham's descendants and specific territories is debated. The biblical account may not accurately reflect historical movements or the ethnogenesis of these groups. Some archaeologists and historians suggest the biblical narrative was retroactively fitted to known historical geography.
