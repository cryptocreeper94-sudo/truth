# C-0011 — CIA conducted illegal mind control experiments on unwitting subjects

## The claim
Project MKUltra (1953–1973) was a CIA program that conducted experiments involving LSD, sensory deprivation, hypnosis, and other techniques on human subjects — many of whom did not consent and were not informed. CIA Director Richard Helms ordered the destruction of MKUltra files in 1973; approximately 20,000 pages survived because they had been misfiled in financial records and were discovered through FOIA in 1977.

## The evidence
S-0018: the surviving files are held by the National Archives. The Church Committee (1975) and subsequent congressional hearings produced sworn testimony. The CIA's own inspector general report (1963) documented the program internally.

## The objection, at full strength
This is fully acknowledged mainstream history. The only objection to the *factual claim* is scope — the surviving 20,000 pages represent a fraction of the original documentation, so the full extent remains unknown. Claims about MKUltra's *current* continuation or its connection to other programs are separate claims.

## What would move this tag
Immovable at DOCUMENTED. The U.S. government itself has acknowledged this in congressional hearings, official apologies, and declassified records.
