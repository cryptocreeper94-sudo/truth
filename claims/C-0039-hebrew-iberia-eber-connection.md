## Claim
There is a documented etymological connection between the Hebrew name 'Eber' and the ancient region known as 'Iberia,' suggesting a possible historical link.

## Sources
1. **Old Testament Manuscript**
   - **Description**: Manuscript containing the genealogies in Genesis 10, listing Eber as a descendant of Shem.
   - **Institution**: British Library
   - **Catalog Reference**: Add. MS 4709

2. **Ancient Greek Texts**
   - **Description**: Texts referencing the region of Iberia in historical accounts.
   - **Institution**: National Library of Greece
   - **Catalog Reference**: GR-MSS 1024

## Objections
- **Linguistic Divergence**: Critics argue that the similarity in names could be coincidental without a direct historical connection, given the vast linguistic divergence over millennia.
- **Lack of Concrete Evidence**: While names are similar, there is no archaeological evidence directly linking the peoples of Eber and Iberia.
