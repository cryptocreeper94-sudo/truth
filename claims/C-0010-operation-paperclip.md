# C-0010 — 1,600+ German scientists imported, records altered

## The claim
Under Operation Paperclip (originally Operation Overcast), the Joint Intelligence Objectives Agency recruited over 1,600 German scientists, engineers, and technicians — including individuals with documented Nazi Party membership and war-crimes associations — for U.S. government employment between 1945 and 1959. Security dossiers were altered to remove or minimize disqualifying records, a practice documented in the program's own declassified files.

## The evidence
S-0017: the National Archives holds the declassified JIOA files, including the original and sanitized security dossiers. These are available through NARA's online catalog. The existence and scope of the program were officially acknowledged by the U.S. government.

## The objection, at full strength
(1) The program is publicly acknowledged — it is not hidden history. (2) The Cold War context meant the U.S. faced a genuine strategic imperative: these scientists would otherwise have gone to the Soviet Union. (3) Not all participants had meaningful Nazi involvement; many were apolitical engineers. (4) The program produced significant scientific advances including the U.S. space program. The moral tradeoff is debatable, but the program's existence is mainstream history.

## What would move this tag
Stable at DOCUMENTED. The program's existence, the record alteration, and the numbers are not disputed by any party including the U.S. government.
