## Claim
The descendants of Shem, as mentioned in Genesis 10 and 1 Chronicles 1, correspond to specific territories and historically attested peoples in the ancient Near East, including Elam, Asshur, Arphaxad, Lud, and Aram.

## Sources
- **Genesis 10** in the Hebrew Bible, detailing the genealogies and associated territories (S-0130).
- **1 Chronicles 1** in the Hebrew Bible, corroborating the genealogies (S-0131).

## Objections
The identification of biblical names with historical territories is subject to interpretation. Some scholars argue that these associations are based on later anachronistic readings or may have been influenced by political or theological motives of the authors of these texts.
