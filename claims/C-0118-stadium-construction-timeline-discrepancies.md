# C-0118 — Stadium Construction Timeline Discrepancies

## The claim
Photographic evidence from the construction of major stadiums in the late 19th century shows construction phases completed in a timeline inconsistent with official reports, suggesting advanced technology or labor resources that were not publicly acknowledged.

## The evidence
1. **Photographic documentation**: Photos from the National Archives, catalog number 150-6-12, reveal stages of construction for the 1897 Polo Grounds in New York, showing significant progress in a matter of months.
2. **Official reports**: City planning documents state that construction began in early 1896 and was set to be completed by mid-1897, which contradicts the rapid progress shown in the photos.

## Objection at full strength
Opponents argue that the photographs might have been staged or selectively taken to showcase progress, and that official timelines may include factors like design adjustments and regulatory approvals that are not visually documented in the photos.
