The Septuagint and the Samaritan Pentateuch exhibit significant textual differences, particularly in the Pentateuch. One of the most notable differences is found in the narrative of Mount Gerizim versus Mount Ebal.

**Example Variant:**
- **Deuteronomy 27:4**: The Samaritan Pentateuch refers to building an altar on Mount Gerizim, while the Septuagint follows the Masoretic Text in mentioning Mount Ebal.

**Manuscript Citations:**
- Septuagint: Codex Vaticanus, Vatican Library, Rome, Shelfmark Vat.gr.1209. [Codex Vaticanus](https://digi.vatlib.it/view/MSS_Vat.gr.1209)
- Samaritan Pentateuch: Abisha Scroll, Samaritan Synagogue, Nablus, Palestine. [Abisha Scroll](https://www.samaritans-museum.com/samaritan-torah-scrolls/)

These variations reflect cultural and theological divergences between Jewish sects during antiquity.
