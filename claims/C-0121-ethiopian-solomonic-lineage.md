# C-0121 — Ethiopian Solomonic Lineage through Kebra Nagast

## The claim
The Kebra Nagast asserts that the Ethiopian royal lineage is directly descended from Solomon and the Queen of Sheba, tracing back to the biblical genealogies. This claim connects the Ethiopian Solomonic dynasty to Shem's lineage and has historical implications regarding the claim to legitimacy.

## The evidence
1. **Source:** Kebra Nagast (The Glory of Kings).
   - **Holder:** Bodleian Library, University of Oxford.
   - **Date of Object:** ~14th Century AD.
   - **Verification URL:** https://www.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/ 

2. **Source:** The Book of Genesis, Hebrew Bible.
   - **Holder:** Vatican Library.
   - **Date of Object:** ~3rd Century BC.
   - **Verification URL:** https://www.vaticanlibrary.va/ 

## Objection at full strength
Skeptics question the historical veracity of the Kebra Nagast, suggesting that it was written to legitimize the rule of Ethiopian kings and may contain mythological elements rather than factual genealogies.
