The Babylonian Epic of Gilgamesh, particularly Tablet XI, contains a flood narrative featuring Utnapishtim. This narrative shares parallels with the Sumerian Ziusudra and Atrahasis accounts. The text is preserved in the British Museum on a clay tablet from Nineveh.
