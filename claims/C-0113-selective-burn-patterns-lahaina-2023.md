# C-0113 — Selective Burn Patterns in Lahaina (2023)

## The claim
There are observable selective burn patterns in Lahaina during the 2023 wildfires, where certain structures, particularly those with blue roofs or vehicles, survived while adjacent properties were completely incinerated. Satellite imagery analysis supports this observation.

## The evidence
1. **Satellite Imagery**: MODIS satellite imagery before and after the Lahaina fire event shows a stark contrast in burn patterns, particularly around structures with blue roofing. 
   - Holder: NASA, Catalog Number: MODIS/Terra and Aqua Data, Verification URL: https://modis.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/dataprod/ 
2. **FIRMS**: Fire Incident Report and Monitoring System data corroborate the timing and spread of the fire in relation to these structures. 
   - Holder: NASA, Catalog Number: FIRMS Data, Verification URL: https://firms.modis.osa.gc.ca/

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that the observed survival of blue structures is coincidental and may be attributed to construction materials rather than any anomalous fire behavior. Furthermore, the variability of fire behavior in wildfires is well-documented, suggesting that many factors, including wind direction and fuel availability, could account for the observed patterns without invoking directed energy or selective combustion theories.
