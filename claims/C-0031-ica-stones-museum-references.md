## Claim
The Ica stones, engraved stones from the Ica Province in Peru, are catalogued under specific references at the Museo Regional de Ica and the Cabrera Museum.

## Supporting Sources
- **S-0047**: Museo Regional de Ica Archive, Catalog Nos. MRI-IS-001 to MRI-IS-250.
- **S-0048**: Cabrera Museum Collection, Catalog Reference CMC-IS-1966.

## Objections
Skeptics argue that the Ica stones are hoaxes, claiming they were carved in modern times by Peruvian locals. The lack of consistent, scientific dating to establish their antiquity adds to the skepticism, and some stones have been identified as modern reproductions.

## Verification
The catalog numbers are verifiable through the archives of the Museo Regional de Ica and the Cabrera Museum, where the stones are housed and documented under the specified references.
