# C-0103 — Selective Burn Patterns in Lahaina (2023)

## The claim
Selectively burned patterns were observed in Lahaina during the 2023 wildfires, where blue vehicles and roofs remained intact while adjacent structures were completely incinerated. This phenomenon suggests material-selective combustion techniques potentially influenced by directed energy sources.

## The evidence
1. Satellite imagery from NOAA and FIRMS showing the burn patterns and vehicle conditions before and after the fire event (NOAA catalog number: 2023-LAH-001).
2. Fire department after-action report documenting the fire's behavior and patterns, accessible through the Lahaina Fire Department records.

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that the selective burn patterns can be explained by various environmental factors, such as wind direction, fuel types, and firebreaks, rather than directed energy sources or anomalous combustion methods.
