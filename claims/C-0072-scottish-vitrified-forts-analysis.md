## Claim
The vitrification observed in the Scottish forts of Tap o'Noth, Craig Phadrig, and Finavon was achieved through a deliberate technique, possibly for fortification purposes.

## Supporting Arguments
- The vitrification patterns are consistent across multiple sites, suggesting a common method rather than isolated incidents.
- Archeological surveys have noted the presence of clay and other materials that suggest controlled heating processes.

## Counter-Arguments
- There are no contemporary records or direct evidence that describe the process or purpose of vitrification, leaving the exact method and intent open to interpretation.
