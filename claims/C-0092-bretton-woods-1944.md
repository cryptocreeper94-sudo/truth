# C-0092 — Bretton Woods Conference of 1944 and Its Monetary Implications

## The claim
The Bretton Woods Conference in 1944 established a new international monetary order, creating institutions such as the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and the World Bank, which have shaped global economic policies and practices.

## The evidence
- **Source:** Bretton Woods Conference Proceedings
- **Holder:** United Nations Archives
- **Date of Object:** 1944
- **Catalog Number:** UN Archives, Record Group 1.1
- **Verify at:** https://www.un.org/en/brettonwoods/

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that the Bretton Woods system favored Western economies, particularly the United States, leading to global economic disparities and questioning the fairness of the IMF and World Bank's policies in developing countries.
