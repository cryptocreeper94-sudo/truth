## Claim
The 1904 St. Louis World's Fair demonstrated a consistent architectural style across its structures, indicating a deliberate design strategy.

## Sources
- Source S-0126: "Official Catalogue of the Louisiana Purchase Exposition", Missouri Historical Society, Catalogue No. MHS-2004, URL: [link-to-scan](https://www.mohistory.org/item/MHS-2004)
- Source S-0127: "Photographic Collection of the Louisiana Purchase Exposition", Missouri State Archives, Archive No. MSA-PH-1904, URL: [link-to-scan](https://www.sos.mo.gov/item/MSA-PH-1904)

## Objections
Some historians suggest that the style consistency was overstated, pointing to variations in building materials and design motifs. However, the official catalogue and extensive photographic documentation support the claim of a cohesive architectural vision.
