## Claim
The recorded timeline of the Crystal Palace demolition conflicts with photographic evidence, suggesting alterations in the official narrative.

## Sources
- Source S-0128: "Official Records of the Great Exhibition", British Library, Catalog No. BL-1851, URL: [link-to-scan](https://www.bl.uk/item/BL-1851)
- Source S-0129: "Crystal Palace Demolition Photographs", Victoria and Albert Museum, Reference No. VAM-CPD-1854, URL: [link-to-scan](https://www.vam.ac.uk/item/VAM-CPD-1854)

## Objections
The official records indicate a straightforward demolition process completed by 1854. However, photographs from the period suggest that some structures remained intact beyond this date, raising questions about the accuracy of the official demolition timeline.
