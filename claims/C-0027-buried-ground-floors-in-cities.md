## Claim

Numerous historical city structures have ground floors that are now entirely or partially buried below current street level, suggesting an alteration in city landscape or significant sedimentary events.

## Sources

- Source S-0039 provides documented evidence of buried floors in the St. Mary's Basilica in Krakow, Poland, where archaeological digs revealed a ground floor beneath the current street level.
- Source S-0040 cites similar findings in the Merchants Exchange Building in Philadelphia, USA.

## Objections

The counter-argument posits that these buried floors are a result of gradual accumulation of street debris, urban planning changes, and natural sedimentation over extended periods. Urban historians often suggest these are common in cities with long histories of redevelopment.
