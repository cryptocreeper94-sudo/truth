# C-0120 — Differences in Chronology between Septuagint and Masoretic Text

## The claim
The genealogical accounts in the Septuagint differ significantly in chronology from those in the Masoretic Text, impacting the perceived timeline of events and genealogies leading from Adam to the post-Flood world.

## The evidence
1. **Source:** The Septuagint, Greek Old Testament.
   - **Holder:** British Library.
   - **Date of Object:** ~3rd Century BC.
   - **Verification URL:** https://www.bl.uk/collection-items/the-septuagint

2. **Source:** The Masoretic Text, Hebrew Bible.
   - **Holder:** National Library of Israel.
   - **Date of Object:** ~10th Century AD.
   - **Verification URL:** https://www.nli.org.il/en/archives/ 

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that variations in translations and textual traditions may reflect theological motivations rather than historical realities, questioning the legitimacy of chronological interpretations based on these texts.
