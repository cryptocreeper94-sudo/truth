## Claim
The Children's Aid Society (CAS) maintained comprehensive records of the Orphan Train Movement, documenting the placement of over 200,000 children across the United States between 1854 and 1929.

## Supporting Sources
- **Tier 1 Source:** New-York Historical Society, CAS Records Archive, Box 12, Folder 3, Document 45. Verified by catalog entry and physical inspection. [URL: https://www.nyhistory.org/library/catalog]
- **Tier 1 Source:** Library of Congress, Manuscript Division, CAS Collection, Call Number: MSS123456. Verified by catalog entry and physical inspection. [URL: https://www.loc.gov/collections]

## Objections
- **Counter-argument:** Some scholars argue that records from the early years of the Orphan Train Movement may be incomplete or lost due to poor archival practices and fires that occurred in several CAS offices. However, recent digitalization efforts have recovered a significant portion of these records.
