## Claim
The genealogical timelines in Genesis as recorded in the Septuagint differ significantly from those in the Masoretic Text, with implications for the chronology of early biblical events.

## Supporting Sources
- Septuagint, Codex Vaticanus (Institution: Vatican Library, Catalog No: Vat.gr.1209)
- Masoretic Text, Leningrad Codex (Institution: National Library of Russia, Catalog No: Firkovitch B 19 A)

## Objection
Critics argue that the Septuagint's extended timelines are a result of Hellenistic influences, and the Masoretic Text represents a more accurate Hebrew tradition. Additionally, variations in manuscript transmission could account for discrepancies rather than intentional differences.
