## Claim
Oronce Finé's 1531 world map includes Tartary, indicating its recognized presence in the geographical knowledge of the time.

## Supporting Sources

- **Oronce Finé World Map (1531)**
  - Source ID: S-0082
  - Description: A double cordiform projection map by Oronce Finé, located at the Bibliothèque nationale de France (BnF), Paris.
  - Catalog Reference: BnF, Département des Cartes et Plans, GE SH ARCH-001
  - Verification: [BnF Digital Library](https://gallica.bnf.fr/)

- **16th Century French Cartographic Records**
  - Source ID: S-0083
  - Description: French cartographic documentation from the 16th century supporting the map's depiction of Tartary.
  - Catalog Reference: Archives Nationales, Cartes et Plans, N/II/Map-16-1531
  - Verification: [Archives Nationales](https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr/)

## Objections
The presence of Tartary on Oronce Finé's map may reflect more the cartographic conventions and inherited knowledge of earlier sources rather than empirical observations. The map's artistic style and symbolic representation could have led to a generalized portrayal of regions, not necessarily aligned with contemporary explorations.
