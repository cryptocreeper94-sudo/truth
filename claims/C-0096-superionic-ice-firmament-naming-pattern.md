## Claim

Multiple documented scientific measurements describe a physical boundary in Earth's upper atmosphere with properties that align with ancient Hebrew, prophetic, and apocalyptic descriptions of the "firmament" (Hebrew: רָקִיעַ, raqia). The same structure appears under different names across eras, with each renaming stripping the previous context.

## Documented Evidence Chain

### 1. Superionic Ice — Laboratory Confirmed (2018–2025)

At extreme pressures (150+ GPa) and temperatures (2,000–6,500 K), water undergoes a phase transition where oxygen atoms crystallize into a rigid lattice while hydrogen atoms ionize and flow freely through the structure as bare protons. The resulting material is simultaneously solid (oxygen lattice), liquid (flowing hydrogen), and electrically conductive. This phase — superionic ice (Ice XVIII) — was confirmed experimentally using diamond anvil cells with laser heating.

On February 12, 2025, *Nature* (Vol. 640, pp. 662–667) published the first experimental observation of plastic Ice VII, the precursor phase to superionic ice, using Quasi-Elastic Neutron Scattering at the Institut Laue-Langevin. Water molecules locked into a body-centered cubic crystal lattice while exhibiting rapid rotational motion — simultaneously solid and liquid.

### 2. Van Allen Belt "Impenetrable Barrier" — NASA Measured (2014)

NASA's Van Allen Probes (launched 2012) measured a sharp, persistent inner boundary in Earth's outer radiation belt. Published in *Nature*, the boundary was described as an "extremely sharp edge" acting as an "invisible shield" or "glass wall." Ultrarelativistic electrons cannot penetrate this boundary. It is located at approximately 7,200 miles (~11,500 km) above Earth's surface.

The inner belt contains energetic protons (H⁺). The outer belt contains high-energy electrons and oxygen ions (O⁺). The boundary between them is described as "impenetrable."

## Counter-exhibits

1. **Pressure objection:** Superionic ice requires pressures of 150+ GPa. At Van Allen belt altitudes, mechanical pressure is near-vacuum. The argument relies on electromagnetic pressure substituting for mechanical pressure — this mechanism has not been experimentally demonstrated.

2. **Altitude discrepancy:** The video source states 7,200 kilometers; NASA's measurement is 7,200 miles (~11,500 km). This is a unit confusion in the popular presentation, not in the underlying data.

3. **Mainstream interpretation:** The Van Allen belt boundary is attributed to "plasmaspheric hiss" — very low-frequency electromagnetic waves that scatter electrons. This is the current consensus explanation.

4. **Ancient text interpretation:** Biblical scholars disagree on whether "raqia" denotes a solid structure or an atmospheric expanse. The Septuagint translation (στερέωμα, stereoma) implies solidity; modern evangelical scholarship often favors an atmospheric reading.
