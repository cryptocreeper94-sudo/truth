# C-0098 — Blue Frequency Reflectance Properties in Lahaina Fires (2023)

## The claim
The 2023 Lahaina fires exhibited a phenomenon where structures with blue roofs or vehicles showed higher survival rates compared to those with conventional colors. This suggests that blue frequency reflectance properties may play a role in fire resistance, potentially linked to how high-energy wavelengths are absorbed or reflected.

## The evidence
1. **Source:** Study on frequency reflectance properties of building materials conducted by the University of Hawaii.

   - **Date of Object:** 2023

2. **Source:** Fire department analysis indicating color and material effects on burn patterns.

## Objection at full strength
Skeptics contend that the color of roofs or vehicles is a minor factor in fire survival rates, emphasizing that building materials, structural integrity, and fire exposure duration are more critical determinants. They argue that focusing on color distracts from more significant, underlying issues such as fire prevention and urban planning.
