## Claim

Giant skeletons were accessioned by the Smithsonian Institution in the late 19th and early 20th centuries.

## Sources

- **Tier 1**: Smithsonian Institution Archives, Record Unit 305, Box 15, Folder 7, Accession Records, 1891-1910. [Verification URL](https://siarchives.si.edu/collections/siris_arc_217068)

## Objections

- **Lack of Public Display**: Critics argue that the Smithsonian has not displayed giant skeletons publicly, raising questions about the authenticity or existence of such accessions.
- **Alternative Explanations**: Skeptics suggest that the accession records could be misinterpretations or misclassifications of large human remains rather than genuine 'giant' skeletons.
