## Claim
The descendants of Ham, as listed in Genesis 10, can be mapped to specific historical territories and peoples, including the Egyptians, Canaanites, and Cushites, as corroborated by ancient records and archaeological findings.

## Supporting Sources
- Papyrus Anastasi I, Egyptian Museum, Cairo (Catalog No: JE 66745)
- Canaanite city records, Tel Hazor (Institution: Israel Antiquities Authority, Catalog No: IAA 1997-205)

## Objection
Skeptics argue that the genealogical mapping is speculative and based on later interpretative traditions rather than direct evidence. The use of ancient records to confirm biblical genealogies is often seen as post-hoc rationalization.
