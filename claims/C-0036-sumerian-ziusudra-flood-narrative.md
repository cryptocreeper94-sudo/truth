The Sumerian flood narrative, featuring Ziusudra, is documented on the Sumerian King List and a fragmentary tablet from Nippur. The narrative describes a great deluge and Ziusudra's survival by building a large boat. The text aligns with later Babylonian accounts such as Atrahasis and Gilgamesh.
