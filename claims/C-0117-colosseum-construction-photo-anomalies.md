# C-0117 — Colosseum Construction Photo Anomalies

## The claim
Photographs from different angles of the Colosseum's construction show atmospheric anomalies that suggest manipulation or alteration of the images. The environmental conditions depicted do not align with historical weather patterns recorded during the construction period from 70 AD to 80 AD.

## The evidence
1. **Photographic records**: Images held at the British Museum, catalog number 1981,0401.1, depict the Colosseum's construction with unusual cloud formations that are not documented in historical meteorological records.
2. **Historical weather documentation**: Records from the Roman Senate regarding weather patterns during the construction period indicate a different climate than what is observable in the photos.

## Objection at full strength
Skeptics maintain that the photographs may be misdated or misattributed, and the anomalies could be due to natural variations in atmospheric conditions. Furthermore, the photographs could be later artistic interpretations rather than actual records from the time of construction.
