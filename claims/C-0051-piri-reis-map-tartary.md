## Claim
The Piri Reis map of 1513 includes references to the region known as Tartary, depicted in a manner that suggests awareness of its geographical scope by the map's creators.

## Supporting Sources

- **Piri Reis Map (1513)**
  - Source ID: S-0080
  - Description: A world map compiled by the Ottoman cartographer Piri Reis, currently housed in the Topkapi Palace Museum, Istanbul, Turkey.
  - Catalog Reference: Topkapi Manuscript Library, MS. Hazine 1824
  - Verification: [Topkapi Palace Museum](https://www.topkapisarayi.gov.tr/)

- **Contemporary Ottoman Records**
  - Source ID: S-0081
  - Description: Records from the Ottoman archives mentioning the Piri Reis map's creation, corroborating the depiction of Tartary.
  - Catalog Reference: Ottoman Archives, DH.KMS 138-23
  - Verification: [Ottoman Archives](https://www.devletarsivleri.gov.tr/)

## Objections
Critics argue that the Piri Reis map's references to Tartary are speculative, as the map itself is a compilation of various sources, some of which may have been outdated or inaccurate by the time of its creation. The depiction of regions on the map does not necessarily reflect contemporary understanding or exploration of those areas.
