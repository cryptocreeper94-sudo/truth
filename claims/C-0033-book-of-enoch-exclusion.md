---
title: "Exclusion of the Book of Enoch from Biblical Canon"
status: "SUPPRESSED-IF-TRUE"
sources: ["S-0050", "S-0051"]
objections: "The Ethiopian Orthodox Church includes the Book of Enoch in its biblical canon, suggesting that its exclusion by other traditions is not universal."
---

The Book of Enoch, known for its detailed descriptions of angelic hierarchies and apocalyptic visions, was excluded from the biblical canon by the early church councils. The most notable references are found in the Ethiopian Orthodox Church, which maintains the text within its canon, contrary to the decisions made by the Council of Laodicea (c. 363-364 AD) and subsequent synods. The exclusion is often attributed to its controversial theological content.
