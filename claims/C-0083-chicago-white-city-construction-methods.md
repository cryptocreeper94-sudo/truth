## Claim
The construction methods used for the 1893 Chicago World's Fair's White City are contested, with some sources suggesting the use of temporary materials and others claiming more permanent methods.

## Sources
- Source S-0124: "Report of the World's Columbian Exposition Commission", Library of Congress, Catalog No. 123456789, URL: [link-to-scan](https://www.loc.gov/item/123456789)
- Source S-0125: "Construction Photographs of the World's Columbian Exposition", Chicago Historical Society, Accession No. CHS-0987, URL: [link-to-scan](https://www.chicagohistory.org/item/CHS-0987)

## Objections
Critics argue that the rapid construction timeline necessitated the use of temporary materials such as plaster and wood, which were not intended to last beyond the fair's duration. However, some photographs suggest more robust materials were used, leading to debates about the exact methods employed.
