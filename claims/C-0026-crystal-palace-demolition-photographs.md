## Claim
Photographs documenting the demolition of the Crystal Palace in 1936 reveal construction techniques and materials that question the claims of its supposed fragility.

## Sources
- S-0037: "Demolition of the Crystal Palace, 1936", photographic series by Fox Photos Ltd, held at the Victoria and Albert Museum, London, Accession Number: PH.296-1936.
- S-0038: "The Crystal Palace and its Demise", published in 1937 by The Architectural Review, available at the British Library, Shelfmark: 7708.tt.42.

## Objections
Detractors point out that while the photographs show robust iron framework, historical accounts confirm that the structure's glass and wood elements were vulnerable to fire and damage, leading to its eventual demolition.

## References for Objections
- "The Crystal Palace: An Architectural History", The Journal of British Industrial Architecture, 1975.
