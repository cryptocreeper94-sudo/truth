## Claim
Several structures in France exhibit vitrified characteristics similar to those found in Scottish forts, suggesting a broader prehistoric use of vitrification technology.

## Supporting Arguments
- Chemical analysis of the vitrified material shows consistent high-temperature exposure across different sites.
- Similarities in construction techniques between Scottish and French sites suggest knowledge transfer or shared cultural practices.

## Counter-Arguments
- The geographical and cultural differences between sites make it difficult to conclusively determine a shared practice over such a large distance.
