# C-0109 — Blue frequency reflectance properties in Oregon (2020)

## The claim
In the 2020 Oregon wildfires, structures with blue roofs or exteriors demonstrated a higher survival rate compared to adjacent buildings, suggesting that blue frequency reflectance properties may play a role in fire resistance.

## The evidence
1. Forensic analysis reports from the Oregon Department of Forestry detailing color and material analysis of surviving structures; catalog number: ODF-2020-FAR-005.
2. Satellite imagery assessments from FIRMS highlighting burn patterns in relation to the colors of roofs, catalog number: OR2020-SAT-003, held by NOAA.

## Objection at full strength
Counterarguments emphasize that the observed survival rates of blue structures can be coincidental and linked to broader building codes and materials used rather than any unique properties of the color blue. Fire resistance is often determined more by material composition than surface color alone.
