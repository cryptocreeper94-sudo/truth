## Claim
Records from the 19th century indicate that baby farms, establishments where infants were housed for payment, were widespread in the UK and documented in local government files.

## Supporting Sources
- **Tier 1 Source:** London Metropolitan Archives, Poor Law Board Records, Reference: PLB/245. Verified by catalog entry and physical inspection. [URL: https://www.cityoflondon.gov.uk/things-to-do/history-and-heritage/london-metropolitan-archives]

## Objections
- **Counter-argument:** While some records exist, it is argued that many baby farms operated clandestinely and without official oversight, leading to incomplete or non-existent documentation. The illicit nature of these operations often kept them below the threshold of official record-keeping, complicating verification efforts.
