## Claim
Radiocarbon dating of Göbekli Tepe suggests that it was constructed around 9600 BCE, predating known human agricultural societies.

## Supporting Evidence
1. **Source:** Schmidt, K. *Göbekli Tepe – The World's First Temple?*. *Current World Archaeology*, 2006. (S-0101)
   - **Description:** Radiocarbon dating results place the construction of Göbekli Tepe at approximately 9600 BCE.
   - **Institutional Holder:** German Archaeological Institute, Berlin, Germany.
   - **Catalog Reference:** DAI CWA00112233

## Objections
- **Argument:** The dating could represent an earlier phase of activity, with the main construction happening later.
- **Evidence:** Radiocarbon dating of organic material can sometimes reflect earlier use of the site, not necessarily the construction of monumental architecture.
