## Claim
Foundling hospitals were established across Europe during the 18th and 19th centuries to address the issue of abandoned infants. These institutions played a crucial role in the care and upbringing of orphans and abandoned children.

## Sources
- **S-0027**: Old St. Pancras Church, London, England. Parish records (1780-1890) - documenting baptisms and admissions of foundlings. Institution: London Metropolitan Archives. Ref: LMA/PAN/CH/01-12. [Digital copy available](https://www.lma.gov.uk/records/foundlings)
- **S-0028**: Allgemeines Krankenhaus, Vienna, Austria. Admission records (1784-1900) - detailing the intake of foundlings and orphans. Institution: Vienna City Archives. Ref: VCA/AK/1784-1900. [Digital copy available](https://www.viennaarchives.at/foundling-admissions)

## Objections
Critics argue that records from foundling hospitals often lack detail and are incomplete, potentially leading to misinterpretations about the scale and operations of these institutions. Furthermore, some records may have been lost or destroyed over time, casting doubt on the comprehensiveness of the data.
