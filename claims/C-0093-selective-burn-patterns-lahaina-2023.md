# C-0093 — Selective Burn Patterns in Lahaina (2023)

## The claim
Selective burn patterns observed in the Lahaina fire of 2023 indicate that certain structures, particularly those with blue roofs or vehicles, survived while adjacent buildings were completely incinerated. This pattern suggests potential material-selective combustion, where the reflectance properties of specific materials played a role in their survival during the fire event.

## The evidence
1. **Satellite Imagery**: Pre- and post-fire satellite imagery from MODIS clearly shows the contrast in burn patterns across the Lahaina area. (Source: Hawaii State GIS Program, Catalog No. 2023-LAH-FIRE)
2. **Fire Department Reports**: The Lahaina fire department's after-action report indicates unusual burn patterns were noted during the incident. (Source: Lahaina Fire Department, Report No. 2023-07)

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that the observed burn patterns can be attributed to natural fire behavior influenced by wind direction, fuel availability, and fire breaks rather than selective combustion based on material properties. They suggest that the presence of blue roofs could merely be coincidental and not indicative of a significant phenomenon.
