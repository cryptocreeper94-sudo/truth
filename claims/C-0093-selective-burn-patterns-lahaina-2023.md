# C-0093 — Selective Burn Patterns in Lahaina (2023)

## The claim
Selective burn patterns observed in the Lahaina fire of 2023 indicate that certain structures, particularly those with blue roofs or vehicles, survived while adjacent buildings were completely incinerated. This pattern suggests potential material-selective combustion, where the reflectance properties of specific materials played a role in their survival during the fire event.

## The evidence
S-0150: Lahaina Fire Department after-action report. S-0151: pre- and post-fire satellite imagery from the Hawaii State GIS Program showing spatial contrast in burn patterns across the Lahaina area. The satellite imagery is Tier-1 in the sense that it is a timestamped observational record, but its interpretation — that reflectance caused differential survival — is the claimant's inference, not what the imagery itself establishes.

## Objection at full strength
Fire behavior science provides a robust ordinary explanation: differential survival in wildland-urban interface fires is well-documented and attributable to wind direction, fuel load, ember cast patterns, structure-to-structure ignition pathways, defensible space, construction material flammability ratings, and fire breaks. The "blue roof / blue vehicle" observation has been debunked in peer-reviewed fire science literature: cool colors do not reduce surface temperature enough to prevent ignition under direct flame or sustained ember exposure. Confirmed survivorship bias — observers count surviving blue structures but do not systematically count all blue structures, including those that burned — is the most likely explanation for the pattern.

## What would move this tag
This would move to CONTESTED if a peer-reviewed fire investigation report (NIST, IBHS, or equivalent) documented an anomalous material-selective pattern after controlling for fuel load, wind exposure, and construction type. It would move to DOCUMENTED only with a reproducible physical mechanism confirmed by independent laboratory testing and field correlation. It would be retired as SPECULATIVE if a systematic count of all blue-roofed structures in the fire path shows burn rates consistent with adjacent structures.
