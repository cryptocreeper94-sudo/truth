## Claim

Ancient tools have been found in geological strata that suggest much older origins than traditionally accepted timelines for human tool use.

## Sources

- **Tier 1**: British Museum, Department of Prehistory and Europe, Accession Number 1925,0109.1, Strata Analysis Records. [British Museum Catalogue](https://www.britishmuseum.org/collection/object/H_1925-0109-1)

## Objections

- **Dating Methods**: The accuracy of stratigraphic dating can be challenged due to potential disturbances in the site, leading to misinterpretation of the chronological context.
- **Tool Identification**: Critics argue that the identification of objects as 'tools' could be subjective, with natural formations or later intrusions being mistaken for human artifacts.
