# C-0130 — Rockefeller Foundation Charter Founding Details

## The claim
The Rockefeller Foundation was established in 1913 with a charter that focused on advancing the well-being of humanity through the promotion of public health, medical training, and scientific research. The foundation was created with a significant endowment from John D. Rockefeller.

## The evidence
- **Source**: Rockefeller Foundation Charter Document, 1913 
- **Holder**: Rockefeller Foundation Archives, Catalog Number RF-002 
- **Verify at**: https://www.rockefellerfoundation.org 

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that the Rockefeller Foundation's influence on global health and education can lead to prioritizing certain policies and practices that align with the foundation's interests, potentially marginalizing alternative approaches and voices in the fields of public health and education.
