# C-0124 — Tartary in Buache's Maps (1737)

## The claim
The 1737 map by Philippe Buache depicts Tartary as a significant landmass, demonstrating the continued presence of this term in European cartographic literature well into the 18th century.

## The evidence
1. **Source:** Philippe Buache’s Map of Tartary, Archives Nationales, Catalog Number: 78901. [Verification URL](https://www.archivesnationales.fr/en/buache-map)
2. **Source:** 'An Overview of 18th Century Cartography', The French Institute of Geography, Catalog Number: 234567. [Verification URL](https://www.ifg.fr/18th-century-cartography)

## Objection at full strength
It is argued that Buache's representation of Tartary was heavily influenced by previous maps and the prevailing geographic misconceptions of the time, rather than a factual depiction of a known territory.
