# C-0016 — Josephus maps Genesis names to 1st-century nations

## The claim
In Book I, Chapter 6 of *Antiquities of the Jews* (c. 94 CE), Flavius Josephus provides a systematic identification of the descendants listed in Genesis 10, mapping them to peoples and territories known in his time. He identifies Javan with the Greeks (Ionians), Cush with the Ethiopians, Mizraim with the Egyptians, Magog with the Scythians, Gomer with the Galatians/Cimmerians, Madai with the Medes, Asshur with the Assyrians, and so on. This constitutes the oldest surviving attempt to create a complete key between the Genesis genealogy and historically attested nations.

## The evidence
S-0022: *Antiquities of the Jews* survives in multiple manuscript traditions (Greek, Latin, Syriac). The text of I.6 is standard in Josephus scholarship and available in multiple critical editions. The identifications listed are directly quotable from the text.

## The objection, at full strength
(1) Josephus was writing propaganda as much as history — as a Jewish aristocrat in Roman service, his identifications served political and cultural purposes. (2) Several of his identifications rely on folk etymology (similar-sounding names) rather than demonstrated genealogical connection. (3) He was working 1,000+ years after the latest possible composition of Genesis 10 and had no access to independent genealogical records bridging the gap. (4) Later Christian and Islamic traditions adopted and expanded his identifications, sometimes contradicting his specific assignments.

## What would move this tag
The textual claim — that Josephus provides these identifications — is DOCUMENTED. Whether his identifications are correct is a separate question and would require independent corroboration from Tier-1 sources (inscriptions, archaeological evidence linking specific populations to specific Genesis names).
