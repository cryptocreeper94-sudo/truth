## Claim

Many historical buildings have basement windows that are sealed, indicating possible changes in building use or external ground level adjustments.

## Sources

- Source S-0041 includes architectural surveys from the Palace of Versailles, France, showing sealed windows below ground level.
- Source S-0042 presents similar findings in the old Dutch colonial buildings in Jakarta, Indonesia.

## Objections

The counter-argument suggests these windows were sealed due to practical reasons such as changes in building usage, safety regulations, or aesthetic redesigns. Experts argue these modifications are not necessarily indicative of historical resets.
