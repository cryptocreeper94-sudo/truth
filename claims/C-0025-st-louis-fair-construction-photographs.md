## Claim
Photographs from the 1904 St. Louis World's Fair provide evidence of the construction stages and techniques used, supporting claims of rapid and temporary construction.

## Sources
- S-0035: "World's Fair Bulletin", Volume 5, 1903-1904, collection at the Missouri Historical Society, St. Louis, Catalog Number: 2003.78.1.
- S-0036: "Official Photographic Company of the Louisiana Purchase Exposition", 1904, photographic album held at the St. Louis Public Library, Special Collections, Call Number: 040.1 L93p.

## Objections
Some argue that the photographs selectively emphasize unfinished structures to highlight progress, while omitting images of completed, more robust constructions, thereby skewing perceptions of the overall construction quality.

## References for Objections
- "Photographic Narratives of the 1904 World's Fair", Missouri Historical Review, 2005.
