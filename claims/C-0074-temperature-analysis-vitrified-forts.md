## Claim
The vitrification of fort walls required sustained high temperatures that could have been achieved using ancient technology, suggesting a deliberate effort to enhance structural integrity.

## Supporting Arguments
- Experiments replicating ancient technology have demonstrated the ability to achieve temperatures sufficient for vitrification.
- Archaeological evidence of ancient kilns and metallurgical processes supports the feasibility of such high-temperature projects.

## Counter-Arguments
- The energy and resources required to sustain such high temperatures are significant, and there is limited evidence of large-scale infrastructure to support this.
