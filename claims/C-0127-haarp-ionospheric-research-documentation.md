# C-0127 — HAARP Ionospheric Research Instrument documentation

## The claim
The HAARP (High-Frequency Active Auroral Research Program) facility has conducted ionospheric research using powerful radio frequency transmitters, with documented experiments available through government records. These records indicate potential applications for weather modification.

## The evidence
- **Document Title**: HAARP Research Overview
  - **Holder**: United States Air Force
  - **Date of Document**: 2014
  - **Verify at**: https://www.arl.army.mil/www/default.cfm?action=27&tid=HAARP

## Objection at full strength
While HAARP's documentation confirms the facility's operation and research activities, it does not provide direct evidence that the technology is being utilized for weather modification or geoengineering purposes. Many research projects focus solely on basic science and understanding ionospheric phenomena.
