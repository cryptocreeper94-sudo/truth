The Phantom Time Hypothesis, proposed by Heribert Illig, posits that approximately 297 years (AD 614–911) were fabricated in the historical record. Illig argues this was done to legitimize the reign of Otto III. Despite its intriguing premise, the hypothesis lacks credible evidence and is contradicted by consistent archaeological, dendrochronological, and astronomical records.
