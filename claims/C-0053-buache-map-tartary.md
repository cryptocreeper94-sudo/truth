## Claim
The 1737 map by Philippe Buache includes detailed depictions of Tartary, reflecting its continued recognition in European cartography during the 18th century.

## Supporting Sources

- **Buache Map (1737)**
  - Source ID: S-0084
  - Description: A map by Philippe Buache showing detailed regions, including Tartary, held at the British Library, London.
  - Catalog Reference: British Library, Maps K.Top.118.49.
  - Verification: [British Library](https://www.bl.uk/)

- **18th Century French Geographical Studies**
  - Source ID: S-0085
  - Description: French geographical analyses from the 18th century that discuss the Buache map's depiction of Tartary.
  - Catalog Reference: Institut National d'Études Démographiques, Geographical Studies Collection, INED 1737-04
  - Verification: [INED](https://www.ined.fr/)

## Objections
Buache's map, while detailed, has been criticized for speculative geography, including hypothetical mountain ranges and river systems based on limited or second-hand reports. Critics argue that such speculative elements might question the accuracy of Tartary's depiction, representing more the ambitions of comprehensive geographic knowledge than empirical certitude.
