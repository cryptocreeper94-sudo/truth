The Greek flood narrative involving Deucalion is documented in several sources, including works by Apollodorus and Ovid. Deucalion and his wife Pyrrha survive a great flood sent by Zeus to cleanse the earth. This narrative has thematic similarities with Near Eastern flood accounts.
