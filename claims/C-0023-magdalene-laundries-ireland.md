## Claim
Magdalene Laundries operated in Ireland from the 18th to the late 20th century, serving as institutions for the reformation and incarceration of 'fallen' women, including a significant number of orphans and unwanted children.

## Sources
- **S-0031**: Dublin, Ireland. Sisters of Our Lady of Charity records (1850-1970) - documenting the admission and daily operations of the Magdalene Laundry. Institution: Irish Catholic Archives. Ref: ICA/SOLC/1850-1970. [Digital copy available](https://www.irisharchives.ie/magdalene-laundry-records)
- **S-0032**: Cork, Ireland. Government inquiry report on Magdalene Laundries (2013). Institution: Irish Government Publications. Ref: IGP/ML/2013. [Digital copy available](https://www.gov.ie/magdalene-laundries-report)

## Objections
Detractors argue that while Magdalene Laundries did house orphans, the primary focus of these institutions was on women deemed as socially deviant, and records may exaggerate the number of orphans due to stigma and sensationalism in reporting.
