Operation Mockingbird was a covert CIA program aimed at influencing domestic and foreign media outlets. The program involved paying journalists and news organizations to publish CIA-approved narratives.

## Sources

1. **Church Committee Report**
   - Institution: National Archives and Records Administration (NARA)
   - Record Group: RG 233
   - Series: Senate Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations with Respect to Intelligence Activities
   - Reference: https://catalog.archives.gov/id/193875

2. **CIA Declassified Documents**
   - Institution: Central Intelligence Agency Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) Electronic Reading Room
   - Document Title: "Family Jewels"
   - Reference: https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp90-00965r000200080008-7

## Objections

- The scope and effectiveness of Operation Mockingbird are contested, with some arguing that media influence was overstated and not as pervasive as claimed.
- Critics note that many journalists deny any direct involvement or knowledge of the program, suggesting that its influence may have been limited to a few individuals or organizations.
