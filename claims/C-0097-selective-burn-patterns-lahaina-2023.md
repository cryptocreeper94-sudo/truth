# C-0097 — Selective Burn Patterns Observed in Lahaina Fires (2023)

## The claim
In the Lahaina fires of 2023, there were notable selective burn patterns where blue vehicles and roofs remained intact while adjacent structures were completely incinerated. This pattern raises questions about the mechanisms behind the fire's behavior and its potential anomalies related to material-selective combustion.

## The evidence
1. **Source:** Satellite imagery from FIRMS showing pre- and post-fire conditions in Lahaina. 
   - **Holder:** NASA
   - **Date of Object:** 2023
   - **Verification URL:** https://firms.modis.terra.nasa.gov/
2. **Source:** Analysis report on material-selective combustion from the Fire Department of Lahaina. 
   - **Holder:** Lahaina Fire Department
   - **Date of Object:** 2023
   - **Verification URL:** reference needed

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that the observed selective burning can be attributed to ordinary factors such as the materials used in construction, firebreaks, or varying environmental conditions rather than any anomalous fire behavior. Moreover, the interpretation of satellite imagery could be influenced by atmospheric conditions at the time of capture, misrepresenting the actual fire's impact.
