# C-0128 — Smithsonian Institution Act of 1846 Founding Details

## The claim
The Smithsonian Institution was established by the Smithsonian Institution Act of 1846, which provided for the creation of a trust to manage the institution and its museums, libraries, and research facilities. This act was signed into law on August 10, 1846, and is based on a donation from James Smithson.

## The evidence
- **Source**: The Smithsonian Institution Act of 1846 
- **Holder**: Smithsonian Institution Archives, Catalog Number 0000-0010 
- **Verify at**: https://siarchives.si.edu 

## Objection at full strength
The legitimacy of the Smithsonian Institution's founding has been challenged due to claims that the institution has been influenced by external parties and interests, particularly regarding funding and governance. Critics argue that this influence undermines its stated mission of promoting knowledge and scholarship independently.
