# C-0106 — Canadian Wildfire Underreporting in US Media (2023-2026)

## The claim
Canadian wildfires during the years 2023-2026 were significantly underreported in US media compared to similar natural disasters, with a timestamped analysis showing a direct correlation between event scale and coverage volume.

## The evidence
1. A report from the Canadian Interagency Forest Fire Centre detailing the scale of wildfires in Canada from 2023 to 2026, held by the Government of Canada. (Catalog No. CIFC-2023-2026, [Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org/web/20230701000000/http://ciffc.ca/report-2023-2026))
2. Nielsen ratings data showing viewership metrics for US media outlets during the reported events, available from Nielsen Media Research. (Catalog No. NM-2023-2026, [Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org/web/20230701000000/http://nielsen.com/nielsen-ratings-report-2023-2026))

## Objection at full strength
The claim of underreporting can be challenged by the argument that US media prioritizes stories based on perceived audience interest and relevance. Therefore, despite the scale of Canadian wildfires, the lower coverage volume might reflect editorial decisions rather than an intentional underreporting.
