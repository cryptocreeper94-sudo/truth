# C-0132 — Impact of GEB Occasional Letter No. 1 (1906) on Education

## The claim
The GEB Occasional Letter No. 1, published in 1906, played a significant role in shaping educational policies in the United States by advocating for a scientific and progressive approach to education, reflecting the ideals of the early 20th-century reform movements.

## The evidence
- **Source:** GEB Occasional Letter No. 1, published by the General Education Board.
- **Institution:** Rockefeller Archive Center.
- **Catalog Number:** 02.02.01.01.
- **Verification URL:** https://rockefellerarchivecenter.org

## Objection at full strength
Some educational historians contend that the GEB's influence was overstated, arguing that many educational reforms were already underway prior to the letter's publication. They point to various social and political movements that shaped education independently of the GEB's directives.
