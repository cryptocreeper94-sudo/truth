The Book of Jasher is referenced in the Hebrew Bible in Joshua 10:13 and 2 Samuel 1:18, suggesting its historical existence as a recognized text. However, the absence of a complete ancient manuscript complicates its authenticity. The earliest known versions of the text are medieval, housed in various European libraries, including the Bodleian Library at Oxford University.
