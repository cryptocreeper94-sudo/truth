# C-0119 — Genealogies of Shem, Ham, and Japheth

## The claim
The genealogies presented in Genesis 5, 10, and 11, as well as 1 Chronicles 1, outline the descendants of Shem, Ham, and Japheth, mapping their names to historically attested territories and peoples. The Septuagint presents a different chronology compared to the Masoretic Text, which affects the understanding of these lineages.

## Objection at full strength
The genealogies in the Bible are often seen as symbolic rather than literal, with debates surrounding their historical accuracy. Additionally, the discrepancies between the Septuagint and Masoretic Texts could suggest that these genealogies were subject to revision or interpretation, undermining their reliability as historical records.
