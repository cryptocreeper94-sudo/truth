## Claim
The Tower of Babel narrative is a contested explanation for linguistic diversification, with some historical texts supporting the event while linguistic evidence suggests a gradual evolution.

## Sources
1. **Sumerian King List**
   - **Description**: Ancient text mentioning the confusion of languages among peoples.
   - **Institution**: Istanbul Archaeology Museums
   - **Catalog Reference**: Ni 3191

2. **Linguistic Phylogeny Studies**
   - **Description**: Comprehensive studies mapping out the family tree of languages, suggesting gradual diversification.
   - **Institution**: Max Planck Institute for Evolutionary Anthropology
   - **Catalog Reference**: MPI-EA-LING-2021

## Objections
- **Gradual Evolution**: Linguists argue for a gradual evolution of languages due to migration and cultural exchange, rather than a single event causing diversification.
- **Historical Plausibility**: Critics question the historical plausibility of the Tower of Babel event, suggesting it may be allegorical rather than literal.
