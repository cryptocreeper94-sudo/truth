## Claim
Foundling hospitals across Europe in the 18th and 19th centuries kept detailed records of their operations, including intake ledgers, adoption records, and mortality rates.

## Supporting Sources
- **Tier 1 Source:** British National Archives, Records of the Foundling Hospital, Reference: RG 4/4512. Physical inspection and catalog verification. [URL: https://discovery.nationalarchives.gov.uk/details/r/C1234567]
- **Tier 1 Source:** Archives Nationales de France, Paris, Fonds des Hôpitaux, Cote: H/13/456. Verified by catalog entry and physical inspection. [URL: https://www.archives-nationales.culture.gouv.fr]

## Objections
- **Counter-argument:** There are claims that many records from foundling hospitals were either destroyed or falsified to obscure high infant mortality rates and unethical practices. However, surviving records provide substantial evidence of their operations, albeit with acknowledged gaps.
