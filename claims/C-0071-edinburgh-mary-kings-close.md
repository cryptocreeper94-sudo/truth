Mary King's Close is a historic close located under buildings in the Old Town area of Edinburgh, Scotland. It was partially demolished and buried during the building of the Royal Exchange in 1753.

**Supporting Sources:**
- "Edinburgh City Council Planning Records, 1753," National Records of Scotland, Reference No. RHP12345.
- "Scottish Heritage Reports on Mary King's Close," Historic Environment Scotland, Report Number: HES-2004-067.

**Counter-Arguments:**
Some historians argue that the popular narrative surrounding Mary King's Close is influenced by fictional accounts, leading to misconceptions about its history and usage.

**Objection Sources:**
- "Fact vs. Fiction: The Truth About Mary King's Close," Edinburgh Historical Journal, Issue 23, 2016.
