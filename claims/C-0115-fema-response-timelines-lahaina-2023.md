# C-0115 — FEMA Response Timelines in Lahaina (2023)

## The claim
The timeline of FEMA's response to the Lahaina fires in 2023 indicates potential inconsistencies and delays in aid distribution that correlate with the severity of destruction in affected areas, suggesting possible prioritization based on structural characteristics.

## The evidence
1. **FEMA Records**: Official FEMA documentation outlines the response times and distribution of aid across Lahaina, showing a discrepancies in aid timelines that coincide with areas experiencing different levels of fire damage. 
   - Holder: FEMA, Catalog Number: FEMA-Timelines-2023, Verification URL: [FEMA Official Site](https://www.fema.gov)
2. **Insurance Claims Data**: Aggregated insurance claims show that properties with minimal damage received aid sooner than those with total loss, which raises questions about assessment criteria. 
   - Holder: National Insurance Association, Catalog Number: NIA-Claims-2023, Verification URL: [National Insurance Association](https://www.nationalinsuranceassociation.org)

## Objection at full strength
Critics suggest that the observed response discrepancies are typical in disaster management, where logistical challenges and the scale of damage can lead to delays. They argue that FEMA's prioritization is based on need and damage assessments rather than any selective criteria related to structural characteristics.
