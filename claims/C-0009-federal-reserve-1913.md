# C-0009 — Currency issuance delegated to a non-governmental structure

## The claim
The Federal Reserve Act (38 Stat. 251, signed December 23, 1913) created the Federal Reserve System and authorized it to issue Federal Reserve Notes as legal tender. The twelve regional Federal Reserve Banks are structured as private corporations with stock held by member banks, though subject to congressional oversight. Prior to 1913, currency was issued directly by the U.S. Treasury.

## The evidence
S-0015: the Federal Reserve Act itself — printed statute, available on GPO and in the Statutes at Large. S-0016: the Federal Reserve's own FAQ page (federalreserve.gov) describes its hybrid public-private structure.

## The objection, at full strength
(1) The Federal Reserve is subject to congressional oversight, its chair is appointed by the President, and it remits profits to the Treasury. Calling it "private" oversimplifies a deliberately hybrid structure. (2) The creation was debated openly in Congress for years — it was not secretive legislation. (3) Central banking exists in virtually every modern nation; the Fed's structure is not unusual in global context. (4) The timing (December 23) is sometimes cited as evidence of stealth, but the Senate vote occurred December 23 with a quorum present and recorded.

## What would move this tag
The statutory facts are DOCUMENTED and not disputed even by the Fed itself. Claims about the *Jekyll Island meeting* (1910) or about deliberate intent to create a debt-based economy are separate cards requiring their own evidence.
