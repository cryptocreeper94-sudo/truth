# Rockefeller Foundation Charter

The Rockefeller Foundation was established in 1913 with a mission to promote the well-being of mankind through the advancement of science, public health, and the arts.

## Source
- **Location:** Rockefeller Archive Center, Sleepy Hollow, New York, USA
- **Reference:** Rockefeller Foundation Records, Record Group 1.1, Series 100, Box 1, Folder 1

## Full-strength Objection
There are concerns that the Rockefeller Foundation's considerable financial influence has been used to shape global policies and agendas in ways that may not strictly adhere to its stated mission. Critics point to instances where its funding priorities appear to align with broader political or economic interests.
