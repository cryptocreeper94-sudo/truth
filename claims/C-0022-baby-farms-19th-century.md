## Claim
Baby farms, where infants were cared for at a price, emerged as a prevalent solution for unwanted babies in the 19th century, often leading to high mortality rates due to neglect and poor conditions.

## Sources
- **S-0029**: New York City, USA. Court records of the trial of Amelia Dyer (1896), a notorious baby farmer. Institution: New York Public Library Archives. Ref: NYPL/CR/AD/1896. [Digital copy available](https://www.nypl.org/archives/baby-farm-case)
- **S-0030**: London, England. Inquest reports on infant mortality in baby farms (1860-1890). Institution: British National Archives. Ref: BNA/INQ/BF/1860-1890. [Digital copy available](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/baby-farms-inquest)
