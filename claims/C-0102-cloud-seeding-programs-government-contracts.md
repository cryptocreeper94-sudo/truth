# C-0102 — Cloud Seeding Programs Government Contracts

## The claim
Various state and national governments have initiated cloud seeding programs under documented contracts to enhance precipitation, with contracts available for examination. Notable examples include Wyoming's cloud seeding initiatives and China's extensive weather modification programs.

## The evidence
1. **Wyoming Cloud Seeding Program Contracts** - Government contracts for cloud seeding operations documented by the Wyoming Water Development Office.
   - Holder: Wyoming Water Development Office
   - Catalog Reference: Cloud Seeding Program Contracts
   - Verification URL: https://wwdc.state.wy.us/
2. **China's Weather Modification Program Documentation** - Official records detailing China's investment and execution of weather modification strategies.
   - Holder: Ministry of Water Resources, China
   - Catalog Reference: Weather Modification Records
   - Verification URL: http://www.mwr.gov.cn/

## Objection at full strength
Opponents of cloud seeding argue that the efficacy of such programs is not well established, and concerns about environmental consequences and ethical implications of weather manipulation persist. Additionally, the transparency of funding and operational results can be insufficient, leading to skepticism about the claimed benefits.
