# C-0107 — Selective burn patterns in Lahaina (2023)

## The claim
The 2023 Lahaina fire exhibited selective burn patterns, with blue vehicles and roofs surviving amid widespread destruction of adjacent structures. This phenomenon suggests material-selective combustion potentially influenced by specific energy wavelengths.

## The evidence
1. Satellite imagery from NOAA (FIRMS) showing before and after fire patterns in Lahaina; catalog number: LAH2023-SAT-001, held by NOAA.
2. Forensic photography documenting burned structures and surviving blue roofs, available through the Lahaina Fire Department, catalog number: LFD-2023-001.

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that the survival of specific structures can be attributed to normal fire dynamics rather than anomalous phenomena. Standard combustion analysis often indicates that buildings with similar materials can have varying outcomes based on proximity and fire intensity, thus countering claims of selective burning based on color or material alone.
