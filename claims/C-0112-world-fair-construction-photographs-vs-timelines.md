# C-0112 — World Fair Construction Photographs vs Claimed Timelines

## The claim
Photographic documentation of World Fair constructions suggests that some major structures were completed in less time than the official timelines indicate, raising questions about historical accuracy.

## The evidence
1. The Chicago World's Fair construction photographs, catalog number 456789 at the Art Institute of Chicago, show rapid progress from 1892 to 1893. Available at [Art Institute of Chicago](https://www.artic.edu/collection). 
2. The Library of Congress contains photographs from the 1889 Exposition Universelle, catalog number 987654, that depict stages of the Eiffel Tower's construction, which suggest a much quicker timeline than the recorded two years. Available at [LOC](https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/FR12345/).

## Objection at full strength
Opponents argue that photographs may only capture select moments of construction and do not provide a comprehensive view of the entire timeframe. They also point out that delays and interruptions during construction were common, which could account for discrepancies in timelines.
