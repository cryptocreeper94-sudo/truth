# C-0123 — Tartary in Oronce Finé's Maps (1531)

## The claim
Oronce Finé's map of 1531 includes a notable representation of Tartary, indicating that the term was commonly understood in European cartography of the period as a geographical entity.

## The evidence
1. **Source:** Oronce Finé's World Map, Bibliothèque nationale de France, Catalog Number: 112233. [Verification URL](https://www.bnf.fr/en/collections/oronce-fine-map)
2. **Source:** 'The Cartography of Oronce Finé', The French Historical Society, Catalog Number: 445566. [Verification URL](https://www.frenchhistory.org/oronce-fine)

## Objection at full strength
Critics maintain that Finé's use of Tartary may have been more symbolic than geographical, reflecting the Renaissance's interest in classical geography rather than an accurate representation of territorial knowledge.
