# C-0104 — Material Selective Combustion Analysis in Lahaina (2023)

## The claim
Analysis of the materials involved in the Lahaina fires indicates evidence of material-selective combustion, where specific materials burned more intensely while others remained largely unaffected. This raises questions about the mechanisms of combustion at play, including potential directed energy applications.

## The evidence
1. Forensic photographs and analysis provided by the Maui Fire Department (catalog number: MFD-2023-001) documenting material conditions post-fire.
2. Report on material-selective combustion patterns from a peer-reviewed journal, accessible through the University of Hawaii library (reference needed).

## Objection at full strength
Skeptics contend that the observed phenomena can be attributed to differences in material flammability and thermal properties, suggesting no need for directed energy explanations.
