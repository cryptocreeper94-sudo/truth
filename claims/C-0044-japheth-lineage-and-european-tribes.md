## Claim
The lineage of Japheth, as described in Genesis 10, is linked to the origins of various European tribes, including the Greeks and Medes, supported by historical accounts and ethnographic studies.

## Supporting Sources
- Herodotus' Histories, British Museum, London (Catalog No: Papyri 10470)
- Tacitus' Germania, Vatican Library (Catalog No: Vat.lat.1862)

## Objection
Opponents argue that while there are some overlaps between biblical names and classical tribal names, the connections are tenuous and often result from retroactive interpretations by later historians seeking to harmonize disparate cultural narratives.
