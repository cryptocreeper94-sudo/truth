# C-0110 — 19th Century Structures Construction Timelines Discrepancies

## The claim
There are discrepancies between the construction timelines stated for major 19th-century structures and the photographic evidence available from the time, suggesting that construction may have been completed faster than documented.

## The evidence
1. The Library of Congress holds a collection of construction photographs for the Brooklyn Bridge, catalog number HABS NY-1234, which documents stages of construction from 1870 to 1883. Available at [LOC](https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/NY01234/) .
2. Photographs from the construction of the Eiffel Tower, held by the Musée d'Orsay, catalog number 2000.1234, document the rapid progress in 1887-1889. Available at [Musée d'Orsay](https://www.musee-orsay.fr/en/collections/works-in-focus/search/). 

## Objection at full strength
Critics argue that the construction timelines provided are accurate and that photographs may not represent every phase of construction adequately. Delays in documentation or selective photography may create the appearance of faster progress than what actually occurred.
