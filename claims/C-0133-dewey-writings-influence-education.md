# C-0133 — Influence of John Dewey's Writings on Education

## The claim
John Dewey's writings in the late 19th and early 20th centuries fundamentally transformed American educational philosophy, promoting experiential learning and democratic citizenship in education, which became cornerstones of progressive education movements.

## The evidence
- **Source:** "Democracy and Education" by John Dewey, published in 1916.
- **Institution:** University of Chicago Library.
- **Catalog Number:** 370.1 D49d 1916.
- **Verification URL:** https://www.lib.uchicago.edu/collex/exhibits/dewey/

## Objection at full strength
Opponents of Dewey's approach argue that his theories were impractical and difficult to implement, particularly in traditional educational settings. They claim that his emphasis on experiential learning undermined the importance of standardized curricula and rigorous academic standards.
