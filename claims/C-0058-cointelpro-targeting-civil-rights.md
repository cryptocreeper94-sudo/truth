COINTELPRO was an FBI program that targeted civil rights organizations and activists, including Martin Luther King Jr., using surveillance, infiltration, and discrediting tactics.

## Sources

1. **FBI Declassified Files**
   - Institution: Federal Bureau of Investigation FOIA Vault
   - Document Title: "COINTELPRO: The Counterintelligence Program"
   - Reference: https://vault.fbi.gov/cointel-pro

2. **Church Committee Report**
   - Institution: National Archives and Records Administration (NARA)
   - Record Group: RG 233
   - Series: Senate Select Committee to Study Governmental Operations with Respect to Intelligence Activities
   - Reference: https://catalog.archives.gov/id/193875

## Objections

- Some argue that the documents only reflect a fraction of the FBI's actions and may not represent the full extent of COINTELPRO's activities.
- Critics point out that the program's impact on civil rights movements is difficult to quantify, and some believe its effects have been exaggerated in retrospect.
