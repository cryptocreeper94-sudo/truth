# C-0125 — Smithsonian Institution Act of 1846 Founding Document

## The claim
The Smithsonian Institution Act of 1846 established the Smithsonian Institution in the United States, providing a charter for its operation and governance, and it aimed to promote the increase and diffusion of knowledge among men.

## The evidence
- **Source**: Smithsonian Institution Act (1846)
- **Holder**: Smithsonian Institution
- **Catalog Number**: SIA RU000216
- **Verification URL**: https://siarchives.si.edu/collections/siris_sic_10196

## Objection at full strength
The interpretation of the Act may be contested based on the argument that the Smithsonian's mission and operations have evolved significantly since its founding, potentially deviating from the original intent as expressed in the charter. Additionally, some critics argue that the institution has been influenced by external political and financial interests that may not align with its stated mission.
