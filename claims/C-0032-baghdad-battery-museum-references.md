## Claim
The Baghdad Battery, an artifact believed to be an ancient galvanic cell, is catalogued at the National Museum of Iraq under a specific reference number.

## Supporting Sources
- **S-0049**: National Museum of Iraq Catalog, Inventory No. NMI-BB-1938.

## Objections
Some historians and archaeologists argue that the Baghdad Battery was not used as a galvanic cell but rather as a storage vessel or for other mundane purposes. The absence of evidence for widespread use of electrical technology in the ancient world supports this skepticism, and no direct evidence of its use as a battery has been found.

## Verification
The catalog number can be verified by consulting the inventory records at the National Museum of Iraq, where the artifact is stored under the specified reference.
