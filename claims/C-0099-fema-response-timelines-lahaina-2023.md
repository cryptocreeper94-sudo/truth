# C-0099 — FEMA Response Timelines Analysis for Lahaina Fires (2023)

## The claim
The response timeline of FEMA to the Lahaina fires in 2023 has been criticized for delays that do not align with standard emergency response protocols, suggesting a potential lack of preparedness or mismanagement in handling the disaster, possibly influenced by unusual fire behavior.

## The evidence
1. **Source:** FEMA after-action report detailing response times and actions taken during the Lahaina fires.

   - **Date of Object:** 2023

2. **Source:** News reports documenting the timeline of events and responses from local authorities during and after the fires.

   - **Date of Object:** 2023
   - **Verification URL:** reference needed

## Objection at full strength
Opponents of this claim argue that delays in FEMA's response can often be attributed to a variety of factors, including resource allocation, coordination challenges, and the scale of the disaster rather than an indication of mismanagement. They point out that emergencies often lead to chaotic situations where response timelines can be skewed by unforeseen circumstances.
