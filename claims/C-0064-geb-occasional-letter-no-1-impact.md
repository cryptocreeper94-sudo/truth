## Impact of GEB Occasional Letter No. 1 on Education Reform

### Claim
The GEB Occasional Letter No. 1, published in 1906, significantly shaped educational reforms in the early 20th century by promoting centralized control and standardization.

### Evidence
The Occasional Letter No. 1 is housed at the Rockefeller Archive Center, catalog reference GEB-1906-OL1. It outlines strategies for centralization and standardization of education across the United States.

### Counter-Argument
Critics suggest that the GEB's role may be overstated, as broader socio-economic changes and other educational influences also contributed to reforms during this period.
