# C-0095 — FEMA Response Timelines in Wildfire Events

## The claim
FEMA's response timelines for recent wildfire events, such as those in Lahaina (2023) and Paradise (2018), indicate significant delays that correlate with increased damage and loss. The response times suggest systemic issues within disaster management frameworks, potentially exacerbating the effects of the fires.

## The evidence
1. **FEMA Reports**: Official FEMA reports show discrepancies in response times across various wildfire events, highlighting delays in Lahaina compared to prior incidents. (Source: FEMA, Report No. 2023-WILDFIRE)
2. **Insurance Claim Analysis**: Analysis of insurance claims indicates that regions with delayed FEMA responses experienced higher losses. (Source: National Association of Insurance Commissioners, Report No. 2023-05)

## Objection at full strength
Opponents argue that FEMA's response times are often dependent on various logistical factors, including accessibility, resource availability, and the scale of the disaster. They contend that delays are not inherently indicative of systemic failure but rather reflect the complexities of disaster response.
