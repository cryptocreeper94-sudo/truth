## Claim

Star forts, characterized by their distinctive bastioned shapes, are found on multiple continents, suggesting a widespread architectural influence or knowledge sharing across distant regions.

## Sources

- Source S-0043 catalogs the Fort Bourtange in the Netherlands, with detailed blueprints and its construction records dating back to the 16th century.
- Source S-0044 includes surveys of Fort Ticonderoga in New York, USA, providing evidence of similar design principles.

## Objections

Critics argue that the presence of star forts worldwide is a result of European colonial influence and the dissemination of military architectural knowledge. The similarity in design is attributed to the effectiveness of the star fort design rather than evidence of a single global civilization or reset.
