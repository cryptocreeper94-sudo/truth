Jason's working thesis (Aug 2026 discussion), which reframes but doesn't replace the book's foundation:

- Core anchor: Jeremiah 8:8 — "the lying pen of the scribes" — the transmitted record itself was altered; nothing shown/told can be trusted at face value. He nearly renamed the repo `thelyingpen` for this reason.
- Geography: suspects biblical geography was relocated — e.g., Mississippi as the Euphrates/Nile, "seven smaller rivers coming together," topology and cataclysm evidence in the Utah/Arizona region. Holds that the modern state of Israel was a political creation engineered to bootstrap prophecy expectations.
- Timeline: believes we may be BEYOND the prophetic timeline as commonly read — Armageddon/"fire from the sky" may have already occurred (cites anomalous fires: homes burned but trees/grass untouched).
- Deception structure: many nested layers ("15, 25 levels") that recur in different forms whichever direction you turn; the ultimate deception is one you're not supposed to see coming — the entire narrative frame (where, when) is the lie, not just the events.
- Mounds/pyramids of the Americas: sees the mound networks as *nodal* — laid out like a motherboard when viewed as a whole; function, not monument. Scale-invariance idea: "in a time of giants a motherboard would be a motherboard" — the same tech at a larger scale. The "New World" is nothing new under the sun (Eccl. 1:9) and is likely far more central to the real story than the accepted narrative allows.
- Cataclysm evidence he cites: Utah structures look molten/melted, some rock faces appear to retain "windows"; ties to fire-from-the-sky/already-past-Armageddon reading.
- Toponymy/cartography arguments: American mountains carry the same names as scriptural ones and their topology/height matches scripture better than the Middle East equivalents; state names trace linguistically to scriptural areas; old maps show California as an island/not solid landmass. The state of Israel = a deliberate play on believers' emotions ("a very dangerous game").
- Provenance argument: the "settlers named it in imitation" counter fails because the namers/recorders (Mormons, who were also Freemasons) were inside the system — every layer of "original names" was written by interested pens; repeated reversals erase the ability to even ask the original question.
- Cataclysm-by-fire evidence set he cites: green/desert glass (India/Vedic wars), San Francisco "meltdown," Grand Canyon as a desolated city, megaliths in Montana/Laos (believes Vietnam-era bombing of Laos targeted megalithic truth), Ron Wyatt's ultra-pure sulfur balls at the Gomorrah sites. Mudflood is included in the book but presented-not-asserted; he suspects mudflood itself is partly a layer of the deception. Flat earth flagged but tabled. Key deception model: "everyone is partially right" — truth scattered across camps so no one holds it whole.
- Aug 2026: experiencing this as a full paradigm shift; book is NOT wrong, but certain points must be made explicitly clear in revision.
- Implication for the book: the foundation stays, but the reveal shifts from "hidden events in history" to "the map and the clock themselves are the forgery."
