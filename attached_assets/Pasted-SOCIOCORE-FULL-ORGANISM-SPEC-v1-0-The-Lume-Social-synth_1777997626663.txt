============================================================

   - Capacity for interaction, connection, presence.
   - Depletion → isolation, irritability, avoidance.

2. IDENTITY COHERENCE FLOW
   - Stability of self across contexts.
   - Depletion → fragmentation, masking, role confusion.

3. RELATIONAL STABILITY FLOW
   - Quality, safety, and predictability of relationships.
   - Depletion → conflict, volatility, withdrawal.

4. COMMUNICATION LOAD FLOW
   - Messaging pressure, social obligations, emotional labor.
   - Depletion → overwhelm, shutdown, miscommunication.

These are continuous interpersonal fields.
