1. LUME-BIO CORE — THE 4 BIOCORE PRIMITIVES
============================================================
These are the four irreducible flows BioCore governs.

1. METABOLIC FLOW
   - Energy production, glucose stability, nutrient utilization, cellular metabolism.
   - Governs vitality, fatigue, and long-term health.

2. CIRCULATORY FLOW
   - Blood flow, oxygen delivery, hydration, vascular stability.
   - Governs endurance, recovery, and organ support.

3. NEUROLOGICAL FLOW
   - Nervous system tone, autonomic balance, cognitive load, emotional regulation.
   - Governs focus, calm, stress response, and sleep quality.

4. STRESS/THERMAL FLOW
   - Stress load, inflammation, body temperature, circadian alignment, recovery pressure.
   - Governs resilience, burnout risk, and healing capacity.

Each primitive is a continuous field over time and can be mapped to measurable or proxy signals (wearables, self-report, clinical data, etc.).

5. BIOCORE ROUTING LOGIC — DETERMINISTIC DECISION ENGINE
============================================================

Inputs:
- Wearable data (HR, HRV, sleep, steps, temp, etc.)
- Self-report (mood, energy, stress, pain, symptoms)
- Context (workload, travel, illness, environment)
- 42 node states (normalized)
- Constraints (user goals, medical constraints, preferences)
- Targets (recovery, performance, stability, healing)

Outputs:
- Daily guidance (do more / do less / hold steady)
- Recovery recommendations (sleep, rest, hydration, nutrition)
- Stress modulation (breathwork, grounding, pacing)
- Risk warnings (burnout, overtraining, flare risk)
- Long-term trend insights (healing vs. decline)

Deterministic Rules (high-level):

1. HARD CONSTRAINT ENFORCEMENT
   - If any node crosses a critical threshold (e.g., S5 Overtraining, S6 Burnout, N1/N2 sleep collapse):
     - Recommend immediate downshift (rest, reduce load).
     - Suppress “push harder” recommendations.
     - Escalate warnings.

2. RISK-WEIGHTED GUIDANCE
   - Score = f(Risk, Recovery, Stability, Trend).
   - High risk + low recovery → strong “slow down” guidance.
   - Low risk + high recovery → “safe to push” guidance.

3. RECOVERY PRIORITIZATION
   - If Stress/Thermal and Neurological flows are degraded:
     - Prioritize sleep, rest, and nervous system down-regulation.
   - If Metabolic and Circulatory are degraded:
     - Prioritize nutrition, hydration, gentle movement.

4. TREND-AWARE DECISIONS
   - BioCore does not react to a single bad day.
   - It looks at short-term and medium-term trends:
     - Is this a spike?
     - Is this a pattern?
     - Is this a collapse?

5. HOLISTIC BALANCING
   - No primitive is optimized at the expense of catastrophic damage to another.
   - Example: performance push is blocked if Stress/Thermal is critical.

6. DYNAMIC RE-EVALUATION
   - Recompute all 42 nodes continuously as new data arrives.
   - Adjust guidance deterministically.

- B (all primitives) are positive
- S1/S2 low

- mode = PERFORMANCE or STABILIZATION
