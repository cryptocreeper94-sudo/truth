TRI-ORGANISM GEOMETRY — MERIDIAN / VERDARA ULTRA / HYDROCORE (v1.0)
Deterministic 2D/3D interaction geometry for three Lume-native physical flow organisms.

============================================================
0. PURPOSE
============================================================
This geometry describes how three 4/42 organisms:
- Meridian (Lume-Energy),
- Verdara Ultra (Lume-Outdoor),
- HydroCore (Lume-Hydro),
are visualized and related in a single coherent 2D/3D frame.

Goal:
- Show each organism as a distinct 4/42 entity.
- Show how their flows interact.
- Preserve determinism and structural clarity.

============================================================
1. HIGH-LEVEL LAYOUT
============================================================
Top-level structure:
- A triangular arrangement of three organisms.
- Each vertex of the triangle is a full 4/42 organism:
  - Top: MERIDIAN (Energy)
  - Bottom-left: VERDARA ULTRA (Outdoor)
  - Bottom-right: HYDROCORE (Hydro)

Center:
- A shared interaction core where cross-domain flows are visualized.

2D:
- Equilateral triangle layout.
3D:
- Slight vertical offsets to emphasize domain layering.

============================================================
2. INDIVIDUAL ORGANISM NODES
============================================================
Each organism retains its own:
- 4-axis core,
- 42-node ring,
- internal color palette.

Meridian:
- Domain: energy flow.
- Palette: electric blues, violets, whites.

Verdara Ultra:
- Domain: outdoor flow.
- Palette: earth tones, greens, sky blues.

HydroCore:
- Domain: water flow.
- Palette: deep blues, teals, whites.

Each organism is rendered as defined in its own geometry spec (4/42 core + ring).

============================================================
3. TRIANGLE EDGES — PAIRWISE INTERACTION CHANNELS
============================================================
Each edge of the triangle represents a **pairwise interaction channel** between two organisms.

Edges:
- Meridian ↔ Verdara Ultra
- Verdara Ultra ↔ HydroCore
- HydroCore ↔ Meridian

Representation:
- Each edge is a semi-transparent band or conduit.
- Within each band, directional flow lines show influence and data exchange.

Edge semantics:

1) Meridian ↔ Verdara Ultra
   - Energy ↔ Outdoor.
   - Examples:
     - Powering outdoor infrastructure.
     - Energy availability affecting route safety.
     - Environmental conditions affecting energy demand.

2) Verdara Ultra ↔ HydroCore
   - Outdoor ↔ Water.
   - Examples:
     - Terrain and weather affecting water flow.
     - Water availability affecting outdoor routing and survival.
     - Flood/drought risk feeding into Verdara’s hazard model.

3) HydroCore ↔ Meridian
   - Water ↔ Energy.
   - Examples:
     - Hydro power generation.
     - Cooling systems.
     - Water availability affecting energy infrastructure.

Flow encoding on edges:
- Line color: which organism is “sending” influence.
- Line thickness: magnitude of influence.
- Line direction: source → target.

============================================================
4. CENTRAL INTERACTION CORE
============================================================
At the center of the triangle:
- A small, composite 4/42-like “meta-core” representing the **combined state**.

Structure:
- 3 mini-axes, one per organism:
  - Meridian axis
  - Verdara axis
  - HydroCore axis
- Each axis length encodes that organism’s global health.
- A small inner triangle connects the tips of these axes.

Color:
- Each axis uses its organism’s primary color.
- The inner triangle is blended from all three.

Purpose:
- Quick visual of tri-organism balance.
- If one axis collapses (critical), the triangle distorts visibly.

============================================================
5. CROSS-ORGANISM NODE LINKING
============================================================
Certain nodes in each organism are logically linked to nodes in others.

Examples:

Meridian ↔ Verdara:
- Meridian: load, capacity, outage risk.
- Verdara: safe weather window, visibility, hazard probability.

Verdara ↔ HydroCore:
- Verdara: terrain type, saturation, flood risk.
- HydroCore: flow rate, saturation, flood probability.

HydroCore ↔ Meridian:
- HydroCore: flow rate, head, hydro potential.
- Meridian: generation capacity, cooling capacity.

Visualization:
- Thin, semi-transparent lines connecting specific nodes across organisms.
- Only show:
  - active links (where influence is non-trivial),
  - or on demand (hover/selection).

Line encoding:
- Color: source organism.
- Opacity: strength of coupling.

============================================================
6. 3D STACKING MODEL
============================================================
In 3D, the tri-organism system can be rendered as a **layered tetrahedral form**:

Option A — Vertical layering:
- Top layer: Meridian (infrastructure/energy).
- Middle layer: Verdara Ultra (surface/outdoor).
- Bottom layer: HydroCore (subsurface/water).

Each organism:
- Slightly offset in Z.
- Connected by vertical “pillars” representing cross-domain dependencies.

Option B — Tetrahedral:
- Three organisms at base vertices.
- Central meta-core slightly elevated.
- Edge conduits rise slightly toward the center.

============================================================
7. ALERT AND ENVELOPE VISUALIZATION
============================================================
Per-organism alerts:
- Each organism’s ring and core behave as defined in its own spec.

Tri-organism alerts:
- If a critical state in one organism propagates to another:
  - The connecting edge pulses.
  - The central meta-core triangle distorts toward the failing organism.

Global instability:
- If all three have critical nodes:
  - The entire triangle outline pulses.
  - The central core flashes a warning color (e.g., amber/red).

============================================================
8. INTERACTION MODES (FOR UI/TOOLS)
============================================================
Hover/select:
- Hovering an organism:
  - Highlights its edges and node links.
- Hovering an edge:
  - Shows which nodes are currently influencing across organisms.
- Hovering the central core:
  - Shows a summary of tri-organism health.

Filters:
- Toggle visibility of:
  - pairwise edges,
  - cross-node links,
  - central meta-core.

Time:
- In 3D time-stack mode, each organism can be extruded in Z.
- Tri-organism interactions can be visualized as evolving over time.

============================================================
9. IMPLEMENTATION GUIDANCE
============================================================
For Claude or any renderer:
- Implement each organism’s 4/42 geometry independently.
- Place them at triangle vertices in a shared coordinate system.
- Implement edge conduits as Bezier or straight bands between organism centers.
- Implement cross-node links as optional overlays.
- Implement central meta-core as a small, blended 3-axis mini-organism.

Constraints:
- Geometry must remain deterministic and reproducible.
- All visual states must be directly driven by the three organisms’ live state vectors.
