## Handoff — Deterministic Verification Engine (DVE) Product

### The Idea
A public-facing platform where users submit any social media video URL 
and receive a governed verification report. Every claim in the video is 
extracted, independently verified against institutional/peer-reviewed 
sources, and returned with honest confidence tags (DOCUMENTED, SPECULATIVE, 
CONTESTED, SUPPRESSED-IF-TRUE, REFUTED). Counter-exhibits mandatory. 
No editorial bias. No opinions. Just evidence.

Tagline: "Stop scrolling, start verifying."

### Proof of Concept (completed 2026-08-11)
We downloaded a 128MB Facebook video (Meta's walled garden bypassed via 
yt-dlp), extracted audio with ffmpeg, transcribed the full narration with 
faster-whisper (Whisper AI), pulled out claims about superionic ice and 
the Van Allen belt, then independently verified every scientific claim 
against Nature (Feb 2025 paper), NASA Van Allen Probes data, and 
Physical Review Letters. Three of four hard science claims verified as 
DOCUMENTED. The interpretive layer tagged SPECULATIVE with full 
counter-exhibits. This was done in one sitting — pipeline works.

### Pipeline
yt-dlp (download, 1000+ platforms) → ffmpeg (audio extraction) → 
faster-whisper (speech-to-text) → Lume-V governed claim extraction → 
source verification engine → 8-invariant governance gate → report
