# S-0007 — Modern KJV (without Apocrypha)

## What it is
Any commercially available King James Version Bible printed after the late 19th century. These editions contain 66 books — 39 Old Testament, 27 New Testament — with the 14 books of the Apocrypha removed.

## Why it is Tier 1
Mass-printed and universally available. The absence of the Apocrypha is verifiable by opening the book.

## What it shows
Direct comparison with S-0005 (the 1611 first edition) demonstrates the removal of 14 books. The table of contents, page count, and book ordering all differ.

## Provenance notes
The removal was gradual. The British and Foreign Bible Society decided in 1826 not to fund editions containing the Apocrypha. By the late 19th century, most Protestant publishers had dropped it.
