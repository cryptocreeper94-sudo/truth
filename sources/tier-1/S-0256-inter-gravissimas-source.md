# S-0256 — Inter Gravissimas Papal Bull

## What it is
*Inter Gravissimas* is the papal bull that introduced the Gregorian calendar reform. It outlines the reasons for the change and the methodology behind the new calendar system.

## Where it is held
Vatican Archives, accessible through official Vatican records.

## Verification
The papal bull can be verified through the Vatican's official website and published records.
