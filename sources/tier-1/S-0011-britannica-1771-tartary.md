# S-0011 — Britannica 1st edition entry on Tartary

## What it is
The first edition of the Encyclopædia Britannica (1771), published in Edinburgh. Volume 3 contains a substantial entry on "Tartary" describing it as a real, populated territory spanning much of northern and central Asia, with geographic boundaries, population descriptions, and political structure.

## Why it is Tier 1
A first edition of a major reference work, held in numerous institutional collections worldwide. The printed text cannot be retroactively altered across all surviving copies.

## What it shows
The 1771 Britannica describes Tartary as existing and populated. Later editions progressively reduce the entry. Modern editions do not contain the term. Comparison across editions documents the disappearance of the term from standard reference.

## Provenance notes
The 1st edition was published by Andrew Bell and Colin Macfarquhar. Multiple copies survive. The digitized version on archive.org can be compared against physical copies in institutional holdings.
