# S-0026 — Kebra Nagast

## What it is
A 14th-century Ethiopian national epic tracing the Ethiopian royal dynasty from Menelik I (claimed son of Solomon and the Queen of Sheba) through the Shem → Abraham → David → Solomon lineage back to Adam. The text served as the constitutional foundation of the Ethiopian monarchy until 1974.

## Why it is Tier 1
Physical manuscripts in multiple institutional collections across continents. The Ethiopian constitution of 1955 is a separate printed document citing the same lineage. These are independent objects.

## What it shows
A continuous genealogical tradition, maintained as a matter of state, connecting an African monarchy to the Shemitic line of Genesis. The 1955 constitution legally required the Emperor to descend from Menelik I — making this the only modern nation-state whose constitution encoded a Genesis-era genealogy as law.

## Provenance notes
The Kebra Nagast was compiled under Emperor Amda Seyon I (r. 1314–1344). European knowledge of the text dates to James Bruce's 1770s travels. Multiple manuscripts survive in Ethiopia and European collections.
