# S-0017 — Operation Paperclip JIOA files

## What it is
Declassified files from the Joint Intelligence Objectives Agency documenting the recruitment of German scientists after WWII. Includes original security dossiers, sanitized versions of those dossiers, and internal correspondence discussing the alteration of records.

## Why it is Tier 1
Government records held at the National Archives. Declassified through official channels. The U.S. government has acknowledged the program.

## What it shows
The files document (1) the scale of recruitment (1,600+ scientists), (2) the alteration of security dossiers to remove disqualifying information, (3) specific individuals' Nazi Party membership or war-crimes associations, and (4) the bureaucratic process by which these records were managed.

## Provenance notes
Declassification occurred primarily in the 1990s and 2000s. The records are available through NARA's online catalog and have been the subject of multiple published studies.
