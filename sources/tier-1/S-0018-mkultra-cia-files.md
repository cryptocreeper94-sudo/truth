# S-0018 — MKUltra surviving files and congressional testimony

## What it is
Approximately 20,000 pages of CIA financial records related to Project MKUltra, discovered in 1977 after FOIA requests. These survived because they had been misfiled separately from the operational files that CIA Director Richard Helms ordered destroyed in 1973. Supplemented by sworn testimony before the Church Committee (1975) and subsequent hearings.

## Why it is Tier 1
Government records held at NARA. Congressional testimony under oath. The CIA itself has acknowledged the program and the destruction of records.

## What it shows
The surviving files document (1) the program's existence and financial scope, (2) the involvement of unwitting human subjects, (3) the types of experiments conducted (LSD, sensory deprivation, hypnosis), and (4) the deliberate destruction of the operational records.

## Provenance notes
The records' survival was accidental — they were filed under financial rather than operational categories. This itself is documented in the congressional record.
