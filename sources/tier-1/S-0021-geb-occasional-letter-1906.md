# S-0021 — General Education Board Occasional Letter No. 1

## What it is
A published document by Frederick T. Gates, chairman of the General Education Board (established 1902 by John D. Rockefeller), outlining the Board's vision for American education, particularly rural education. Contains the widely quoted passage: "In our dreams, we have limitless resources and the people yield themselves with perfect docility to our molding hands."

## Why it is Tier 1
A published document from a named author, held in institutional archives. The Rockefeller Archive Center maintains the original records of the General Education Board.

## What it shows
The document articulates a specific educational philosophy that explicitly envisions shaping populations. Whether this represents benevolent reform or social engineering is a matter of interpretation — the document's text is the primary evidence.

## Provenance notes
The Rockefeller Archive Center in Sleepy Hollow, NY, holds the GEB's records. The Occasional Letter has been reproduced in multiple published works on the history of American education.
