# S-0020 — Smithsonian Institution Act

## What it is
The act of Congress establishing the Smithsonian Institution "for the increase and diffusion of knowledge," funded by the bequest of James Smithson. The act establishes the governance structure (Board of Regents including the Vice President, Chief Justice, and members of Congress) and grants the Institution custodial authority over national collections.

## Why it is Tier 1
Printed statute law. Available on GPO. The institution it created is independently verifiable.

## What it shows
The statutory basis for the Smithsonian's custodial authority over natural and cultural artifacts. The governance structure and its relationship to Congress.

## Provenance notes
Standard legislative record. The Smithsonian's own history division provides context.
