# S-0157 — University of Alaska Fairbanks WTC7 Finite Element Study

## What it is
This study presents a finite element analysis of WTC 7, examining the structural integrity and failure mechanisms that contributed to the building's collapse, challenging the official narratives.

## Where it is held
University of Alaska Fairbanks, Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering.

## Verification
The study is published on the University of Alaska Fairbanks website, with access details for peer-review and documentation.
