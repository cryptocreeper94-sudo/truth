# S-0165 — Satellite Imagery of Lahaina Fires (2023)

## What it is
This source consists of satellite imagery captured before and after the Lahaina fires, providing visual data for analysis of the fire's spread and impact.

## Where it is held
NASA's FIRMS database, accessible online.

## Verification
The satellite imagery can be verified by accessing the FIRMS website and searching for the specific dates surrounding the Lahaina fires.
