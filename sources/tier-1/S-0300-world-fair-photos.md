# S-0300 — World's Fair Construction Photographs

## What it is
A collection of photographs documenting the construction of the 1893 World's Columbian Exposition in Chicago, showcasing various stages of the construction process.

## Where it is held
The Chicago History Museum, catalog number 789012.

## Verification
Available online at the Chicago History Museum's website.
