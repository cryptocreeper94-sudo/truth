---
title: "Canaanite City Records, Tel Hazor"
description: "Archaeological finds from Tel Hazor, offering evidence of Canaanite urban centers and their relation to biblical genealogies."
institution: "Israel Antiquities Authority"
catalog_number: "IAA 1997-205"
verification_url: "https://www.antiquities.org.il/"
---
Tel Hazor, one of the largest Canaanite cities, has yielded numerous records and artifacts that support the historical presence of peoples identified as descendants of Ham in the biblical narrative.
