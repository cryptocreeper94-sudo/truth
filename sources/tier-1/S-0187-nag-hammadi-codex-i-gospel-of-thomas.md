# S-0187 — Nag Hammadi Codex I (Gospel of Thomas)

## What it is
A manuscript of the Gospel of Thomas, part of the Nag Hammadi Library, containing teachings attributed to Jesus that differ from canonical texts.

## Where it is held
Coptic Museum, Cairo, catalog number NH I, 1.
