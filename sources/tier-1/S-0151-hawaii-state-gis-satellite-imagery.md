# S-0151 — MODIS Satellite Imagery for Lahaina Fire 2023

## What it is
Satellite imagery captured before and after the 2023 Lahaina fire event, showing the extent and pattern of burn areas.

## Where it is held
Hawaii State GIS Program, NASA FIRMS MODIS/VIIRS active fire data.

## Verification
The imagery is available for public access through the Hawaii State GIS Program's website.
