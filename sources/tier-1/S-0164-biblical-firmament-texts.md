Three primary texts describing the firmament (רָקִיעַ, raqia):

1. **Genesis 1:6-7** — "And God said, Let there be a firmament in the midst of the waters, and let it divide the waters from the waters. And God made the firmament, and divided the waters which were under the firmament from the waters which were above the firmament."

2. **Ezekiel 1:22** — "And the likeness of the firmament upon the heads of the living creatures was as the colour of the terrible crystal, stretched forth over their heads above."

3. **Revelation 4:6** — "And before the throne there was a sea of glass like unto crystal."

The Hebrew word רָקִיעַ (raqia) derives from the root רקע (raqa), meaning to beat, stamp, or spread out — as in hammering metal into a thin sheet. The Septuagint translates this as στερέωμα (stereoma), meaning "firm/solid structure." The Vulgate uses "firmamentum," from which the English "firmament" derives.
