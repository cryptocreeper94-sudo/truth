# S-0115 — Paracas skulls catalog

## What it is
Catalog listing for the elongated skulls excavated by Julio Tello at the Paracas Necropolis in 1928 and held in the national museum in Lima. The skulls themselves are physical objects on display and in storage.

## Why it is Tier 1
The skulls are physical objects held in an institutional collection; their cranial morphology can be examined directly and cannot be retroactively edited.

## What it shows
The catalog documents that elongated skulls from the Paracas culture exist in institutional custody. It does not document a cause — artificial cranial deformation (head-binding) is the standard archaeological finding, and any claim beyond that must argue from additional evidence.
