# S-0012 — Inter gravissimas (1582)

## What it is
The papal bull by which Pope Gregory XIII decreed the reform of the Julian calendar. The document specifies the removal of 10 days (October 4, 1582 to be followed by October 15, 1582) and establishes the leap year rules still in use.

## Why it is Tier 1
An official document issued by a head of state, distributed to Catholic nations, held in institutional archives. The decree's effects are independently verifiable through surviving dated documents from the period.

## What it shows
The precise mechanism of the calendar change: which days were removed, the mathematical rationale (correcting Julian drift), and the authority by which it was done.

## Provenance notes
The original is in the Vatican Archives. Multiple contemporary copies were distributed to Catholic courts. The text has been published in numerous scholarly collections.
