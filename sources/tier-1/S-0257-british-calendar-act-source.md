# S-0257 — British Calendar Act of 1750

## What it is
The British Calendar Act of 1750 established the adoption of the Gregorian calendar in Great Britain, detailing how the transition would occur and its implications for society.

## Where it is held
The National Archives, UK, accessible through their official website.
