# S-0022 — Josephus, Antiquities of the Jews

## What it is
A 20-volume history of the Jewish people written by Flavius Josephus, a 1st-century Jewish historian and Roman citizen, completed c. 94 CE. Book I, Chapter 6 provides the most systematic ancient identification of Genesis 10 names with known nations. Josephus maps Gomer to the Galatians, Magog to the Scythians, Javan to the Greeks, Cush to the Ethiopians, Mizraim to the Egyptians, and so on.

## Why it is Tier 1
The Antiquities survives in multiple independent manuscript traditions across three languages (Greek, Latin, Syriac). No single institution controls the text. The earliest surviving manuscripts date to the 9th century, with quotations in earlier Church Fathers confirming the text's existence and content centuries before.

## What it shows
The earliest surviving attempt at a complete key between Genesis genealogy and historically attested nations. Josephus explicitly claims personal knowledge and access to sources no longer available to us.

## Provenance notes
Josephus wrote under Roman patronage (Flavian dynasty), which colors his perspective. His works were preserved primarily by Christian copyists, who valued them for their connection to biblical history. The textual tradition is well-studied.
