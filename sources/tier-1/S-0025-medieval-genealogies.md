# S-0025 — Medieval European genealogical chronicles

## What it is
Two representative examples of medieval European royal genealogies tracing ancestry to Japheth. The Anglo-Saxon Chronicle traces West Saxon kings through Woden to Sceaf to Noah to Japheth. The Lebor Gabála Érenn traces the Gaels through Japheth via a complex migration narrative.

## Why it is Tier 1
Physical manuscripts held in institutional collections. Multiple recensions exist for both texts, allowing comparison.

## What it shows
That the practice of tracing European ruling lineages to Japheth was widespread and systematic across cultures that had no direct contact with each other's genealogical traditions. The claim itself — not its accuracy — is documented.

## Provenance notes
The ASC exists in seven manuscripts, the earliest (A) held at Corpus Christi College, Cambridge. The LGÉ was compiled in the 12th century from earlier sources; multiple manuscripts survive.
