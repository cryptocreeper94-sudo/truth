# S-0255 — Codex Vaticanus

## What it is
An early manuscript of the Bible that includes the Tetragrammaton in Greek characters, suggesting its original preservation in some translations.

## Where it is held
Vatican Library, Vatican City, Catalog Number: Vat. Gr. 1209.

## Verification
The existence and details of Codex Vaticanus can be verified through the Vatican Library's official website.
