Millot, M., et al. "Nanosecond X-ray diffraction of shock-compressed superionic water ice." *Nature*, 2019. First experimental evidence of superionic ice (Ice XVIII) using laser-driven shock compression. Oxygen atoms form a face-centered cubic lattice while hydrogen ions (protons) flow freely through the structure, creating electrical conductivity. Confirmed at pressures of ~100-400 GPa and temperatures of ~2,000-3,000 K. Subsequent diamond anvil cell experiments (2020-2025) confirmed the phase at static conditions up to 150 GPa and 6,500 K.
