# S-0116 — Julio Tello's Paracas excavation report

## What it is
Julio C. Tello, the founder of Peruvian archaeology, excavated the Paracas Necropolis beginning in 1927–1928 and published findings on the burials and cranial material. His excavation papers are archived at the Universidad Nacional Mayor de San Marcos.

## Why it is Tier 1
A contemporary excavation record written by the excavator at the time of the dig, preserved in an institutional archive.

## What it shows
Tello's documentation of the Paracas burials, textiles, and cranial material, including elongated skulls that he attributed to intentional cranial deformation practices.
