# S-0008 — Children's Aid Society Annual Reports

## What it is
The official annual reports of the Children's Aid Society, founded by Charles Loring Brace in 1853. These reports document the scope, numbers, destinations, and methods of the orphan train program year by year, including the number of children "placed out" and the locations they were sent to.

## Why it is Tier 1
Institutional records published contemporaneously. Held in archival collections. The numbers and methods described are the organization's own reporting.

## What it shows
The reports document the scale (estimated 250,000 children over 75 years), the mechanics (children transported by rail, displayed for selection by local families), and the stated rationale (removing children from urban poverty).

## Provenance notes
The Society continues to exist (as Children's Aid) and has not disputed these records or the program's scale.
