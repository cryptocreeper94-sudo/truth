# S-0003 — The New Madrid claims files themselves

## What it is
The working records of the U.S. Recorder of Land Titles for Missouri Territory processing claims under the 1815 relief act (S-0002): sworn witness statements that specific tracts were earthquake-injured, issued certificates, locations, and the litigation trail that followed for forty years.

## Why it is Tier 1
Original manuscript government records held in a state archive with a published finding aid; the derivative land patents are independently recorded in the federal General Land Office system. Two independent record systems (state archive + federal land patents) would both have to be altered to change the story they tell.

## What it shows
A reader can verify: individual, named, sworn, dated attestations of earthquake-destroyed land — the granular evidence layer beneath the statute's general finding. Also documents the notorious speculation in New Madrid certificates (claims traded and abused), which is itself contemporaneously recorded.

## Provenance notes
The finding aid is public; the records are boring in exactly the trustworthy way — administrative paper generated for property purposes, not narrative purposes. The speculation/fraud surrounding certificates means any *individual* claim file must be read with that context; the fraud is documented in the same record system.
