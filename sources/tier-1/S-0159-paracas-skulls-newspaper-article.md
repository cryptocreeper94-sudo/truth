# S-0159 — Newspaper reporting on the Paracas skulls

## What it is
Contemporary newspaper coverage of Julio Tello's Paracas excavations and the elongated skulls. Filed as Tier 2 (contemporary record) — it is what was written at the time, not a physical survivor in the Tier-1 sense.

## Why it is Tier 2
Newspapers of the period exist in scattered physical and microfilm copies across many institutions; the reporting reflects what was said at the time of discovery.

## What it shows
That the Paracas discoveries were publicly reported when they happened. The content of period reporting is evidence of contemporary knowledge, not of anatomical fact.
