institution: "University of Munich Library"
catalog_reference: "Illig, H. (1991). Das erfundene Mittelalter."

---
Heribert Illig's 'Das erfundene Mittelalter' argues for a fabricated historical period in the Early Middle Ages. The hypothesis is critically reviewed in academic circles, with emphasis on its lack of empirical support and counter-evidence from established historical and scientific methods.
