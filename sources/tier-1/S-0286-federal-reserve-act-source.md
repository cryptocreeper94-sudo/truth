# S-0286 — Federal Reserve Act of 1913

## What it is
The Federal Reserve Act is a significant piece of legislation that created the Federal Reserve System, aimed at providing the country with a safer, more flexible, and more stable monetary and financial system.

## Where it is held
Library of Congress, Washington, D.C.
