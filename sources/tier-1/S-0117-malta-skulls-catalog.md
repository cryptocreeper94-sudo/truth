# S-0117 — Malta skulls catalog

## What it is
Catalog listing for skulls recovered from the Ħal-Saflieni Hypogeum, including examples with elongated (dolichocephalic) morphology, held by Heritage Malta.

## Why it is Tier 1
The skulls are physical objects in institutional custody; their morphology can be directly examined.

## What it shows
That skulls from the Hypogeum exist in the national collection and that some display unusual cranial form. Interpretation (natural variation, pathology, artificial deformation) belongs in claim cards. Public display of the skulls has been intermittent, which is itself a documented fact and not evidence of suppression without Tier-5-grade support.

## Provenance notes
The daemon-recorded catalog reference (NMA 1933/87) and the original verification URL (heritagemalta.org/archaeology/malta-skulls) could not be confirmed — the URL pattern does not match Heritage Malta's current site. The verify-at link above points to the museum's official page. The catalog reference needs confirmation before claims rely on it.
