# S-0009 — National Orphan Train Complex

## What it is
A museum and research center in Concordia, Kansas, dedicated to preserving the history of the orphan train movement. Holds rider records, original photographs, placement documents, and recorded first-person testimonies from surviving riders and their descendants.

## Why it is Tier 1
Physical records, photographs, and testimony from documented participants. The institution exists specifically to preserve this material.

## What it shows
Individual rider stories, including cases where children had living parents or relatives. Placement records showing the mechanics of the selection process. Photographic evidence of the children and the process.

## Provenance notes
The Complex was established in 2007. Its collections include donations from rider families and materials transferred from other archives.
