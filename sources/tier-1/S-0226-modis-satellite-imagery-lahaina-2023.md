# S-0226 — MODIS Satellite Imagery for Lahaina (2023)

## What it is
Satellite imagery captured by the MODIS (Moderate Resolution Imaging Spectroradiometer) sensors aboard NASA's Terra and Aqua satellites, providing before and after images of the Lahaina fire event.

## Where it is held
NASA, MODIS Data Catalog, accessible at https://modis.gsfc.nasa.gov/data/dataprod/

## Verification
The satellite imagery can be independently verified by accessing the MODIS data catalog and searching for Lahaina fire event images.
