# S-0024 — 1 Chronicles 1

## What it is
The first chapter of 1 Chronicles provides a genealogy from Adam through Noah's three sons, through the Table of Nations (paralleling Genesis 10), and then through the line of Shem to Abraham, Isaac, Jacob, and the twelve tribes of Israel, then through the line of Esau/Edom. It is the most condensed single-chapter genealogy in the Hebrew Bible.

## Why it is Tier 1
Part of the Leningrad Codex (S-0004). A physical manuscript held in an institutional collection. The text can be compared word-by-word with Genesis 5, 10, and 11 to identify consistencies and variations.

## What it shows
A parallel genealogical tradition that can be cross-referenced with Genesis. Minor variations between the two (spelling differences, name ordering) document the transmission history. The genealogy's inclusion in Chronicles — a text focused on David's kingdom — connects the universal genealogy (all nations) to the particular genealogy (Israel) through Abraham.

## Provenance notes
1 Chronicles is generally dated to the Persian period (5th-4th century BCE). Its relationship to Genesis is debated — it may draw from Genesis, share a common source, or reflect an independent tradition.
