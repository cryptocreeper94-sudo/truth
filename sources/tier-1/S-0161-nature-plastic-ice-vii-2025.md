Rescigno, M., Bove, L.E., et al. "Experimental observation of the plastic phase of ice VII." *Nature*, Volume 640, pages 662–667 (February 12, 2025). First direct experimental confirmation of plastic Ice VII using Quasi-Elastic Neutron Scattering (QENS). Water molecules locked in BCC crystal lattice while exhibiting rapid rotational motion at pressures >4 GPa.
