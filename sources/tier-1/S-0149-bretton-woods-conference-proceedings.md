# S-0149 — Bretton Woods Conference proceedings

## What it is
The official proceedings and documents of the United Nations Monetary and Financial Conference held at Bretton Woods, New Hampshire, July 1–22, 1944, including the Final Act and the Articles of Agreement of the IMF and IBRD. Published by the U.S. Department of State in 1948 in two volumes.

## Why it is Tier 1
A government publication printed and distributed in 1948, held in federal depository libraries across the United States and digitized in full by the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis. Retroactive alteration would have to reach every depository copy.

## What it shows
The complete documentary record of the conference: delegate lists, committee reports, the Final Act, and the Articles of Agreement establishing the IMF and the World Bank. A reader can verify every institutional claim about the conference directly against the scanned pages.
