# S-0019 — Seattle Underground

## What it is
A network of underground passageways and preserved storefronts beneath the streets of Pioneer Square in Seattle, Washington. These were ground-level structures before the city regraded its streets by one to two stories following the Great Seattle Fire of June 6, 1889. The original ground floors became basements; new entrances were built at the new street level.

## Why it is Tier 1
A physical, visitable structure. The city's regrading records are held in municipal archives. The buildings themselves are the evidence.

## What it shows
That an entire urban district was buried beneath a new street grade within documented memory. The original architecture — windows, doors, storefronts — is visible at the sub-street level.

## Provenance notes
The underground was rediscovered and opened for tours by Bill Speidel in 1965. The fire and regrading are exhaustively documented in Seattle municipal records.
