# S-0016 — Federal Reserve FAQ

## What it is
The Federal Reserve's own publicly available FAQ page, which describes the System's governance structure, including the relationship between the Board of Governors (government agency) and the twelve regional Federal Reserve Banks (structured as private corporations with stock held by member banks).

## Why it is Tier 1
An institutional self-description, publicly accessible. What the institution says about itself is a primary source for its own structure, regardless of interpretation.

## What it shows
The Fed's own language describes a hybrid structure that is neither purely public nor purely private. The regional banks issue stock to member banks. The Board of Governors is a government agency.

## Provenance notes
Live web page maintained by the Federal Reserve Board. Archived versions available through the Wayback Machine.
