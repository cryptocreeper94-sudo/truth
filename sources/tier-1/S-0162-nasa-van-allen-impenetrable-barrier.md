Baker, D.N., et al. "An impenetrable barrier to ultrarelativistic electrons in the Van Allen radiation belts." *Nature*, November 2014. NASA's Van Allen Probes measured a sharp, persistent inner boundary in the outer radiation belt at approximately 7,200 miles (~11,500 km) altitude. Described as an "extremely sharp edge" that ultrarelativistic electrons cannot penetrate. Attributed to plasmaspheric hiss (VLF electromagnetic waves).
