# S-0276 — FEMA BPAT Report

## What it is
A detailed report analyzing the performance of the World Trade Center buildings during and after the September 11 attacks, with a focus on structural integrity and fire dynamics.

## Where it is held
National Institute of Standards and Technology, catalog number: NIST SP 800-34.

## Verification
The report is accessible via the NIST website, providing independent verification of its existence.
