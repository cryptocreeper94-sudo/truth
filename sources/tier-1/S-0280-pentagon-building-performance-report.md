# S-0280 — Pentagon Building Performance Report

## What it is
A report detailing the examination of the Pentagon’s structural performance during the September 11 attack, with emphasis on damage assessments and impact analysis.

## Where it is held
National Institute of Standards and Technology, catalog number: NIST NCSTAR 3-1.

## Verification
The report is available on the NIST website for independent verification.
