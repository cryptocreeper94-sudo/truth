# S-0010 — David Rumsey Map Collection

## What it is
One of the largest private map collections in the world, now housed at Stanford. Over 130,000 high-resolution scans of maps, atlases, and cartographic materials spanning the 16th through 20th centuries. Searchable by date, cartographer, region, and keyword.

## Why it is Tier 1
Physical maps held in an institutional collection, digitized at high resolution. The objects cannot be retroactively altered across both the physical and digital records. Multiple cartographers working independently provide cross-verification.

## What it shows
Searchable for any geographic term across centuries. A search for "Tartary" returns dozens of results across multiple centuries. State-by-state comparison of the same region shows how cartographic depictions changed over time.

## Provenance notes
David Rumsey began collecting in the 1980s. The collection was donated to Stanford in 2022. Provenance of individual items is tracked in the catalog metadata.
