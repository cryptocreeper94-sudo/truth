# S-0158 — Smithsonian accession records (Record Unit 305)

## What it is
Accession records from the Smithsonian Institution documenting items received between 1891 and 1910. Record Unit 305 is the United States National Museum's accession record series and is a real, cataloged archival unit.

## Why it is Tier 1
Contemporary institutional ledgers, created at time of receipt, held in an archival unit with a documented finding aid. Retroactive alteration would require modifying bound ledgers under archival custody.

## What it shows
Whatever the ledgers record — item descriptions, dates, and provenance as written at time of accession. Specific box/folder/page citations to "giant" skeletal entries (e.g., Box 15, Folder 7) were generated by the research daemon and have NOT been confirmed against the actual finding aid. No claim may treat those specific citations as verified until someone has examined the records or the finding aid confirms them.

## Provenance notes
Record Unit 305 is genuine and searchable via the Smithsonian Institution Archives. The daemon-cited box/folder/page numbers are unconfirmed and should be treated as leads, not evidence. This distinction is the difference between this source supporting a SPECULATIVE claim and a DOCUMENTED one.
