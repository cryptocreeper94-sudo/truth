# S-0285 — Piri Reis Map (1513)

## What it is
The Piri Reis map is a world map created by the Ottoman admiral and cartographer Piri Reis in 1513, notable for its depiction of parts of Europe, Africa, and the Americas, as well as the area identified as Tartary.

## Where it is held
It is held at the Topkapi Palace Museum, catalog number 1153, Istanbul, Turkey.

## Verification
The existence of the map can be verified through the museum’s official website and the Wikimedia Commons page.
