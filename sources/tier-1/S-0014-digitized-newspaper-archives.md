# S-0014 — Digitized U.S. newspaper archives

## What it is
Searchable digital archives of historical American newspapers, including the Library of Congress's Chronicling America project (free, public) and the commercial newspapers.com database. Together they cover tens of millions of pages from thousands of publications spanning the 19th and early 20th centuries.

## Why it is Tier 1
The original newspapers are physical objects held in library collections. The digitized versions are scans of those objects, not transcriptions. The original publication dates and page contexts are preserved.

## What it shows
Searchable for any term. Relevant to this archive: searches for terms like "giant skeleton," "giant bones," "mound builders," and similar return hundreds of individually dated, attributed reports across multiple states and publications.

## Provenance notes
Chronicling America is a project of the Library of Congress and the National Endowment for the Humanities. Newspapers.com is operated by Ancestry.com. Both draw from institutional microfilm collections.
