# S-0015 — Federal Reserve Act

## What it is
The act of Congress that created the Federal Reserve System and authorized the issuance of Federal Reserve Notes. Signed by President Woodrow Wilson on December 23, 1913.

## Why it is Tier 1
Printed statute law, available on GPO and in the Statutes at Large. Its effects are independently observable (the Federal Reserve exists).

## What it shows
The specific mechanism by which currency issuance was transferred from direct Treasury operations to the Federal Reserve System, including the structure of the twelve regional banks and their governance.

## Provenance notes
Standard legislative record. Not disputed by any party.
