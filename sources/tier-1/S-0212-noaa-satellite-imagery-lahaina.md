# S-0212 — NOAA Satellite Imagery of Lahaina (2023)

## What it is
Satellite imagery collected by NOAA during and after the Lahaina fire event in 2023, providing visual data on burn patterns.

## Where it is held
NOAA, catalog number: LAH2023-SAT-001.

## Verification
Available at NOAA's FIRMS website, which provides access to real-time satellite imagery data.
