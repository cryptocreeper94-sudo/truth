### The Problem

Your car is wasting fuel right now. Not because it's broken — because it's never been optimized for how you actually drive, where you actually drive, and what your engine actually needs today.

### What Lume-Auto Does

Lume-Auto connects to your car's OBD-II diagnostic port — a standard outlet under the dashboard, present in every vehicle sold since 1996 — and reads your engine's live data stream. It watches 42 different signals simultaneously: air flow, fuel flow, combustion timing, engine load, driving behavior, component health, and more.

It runs all of those signals through a deterministic governance model built on the Lume 4/42 architecture — the same framework that governs HydroCore industrial water systems and Meridian wireless energy networks. Then it tells you exactly what's costing you fuel and exactly what to do about it.

No guessing. No generic tips. Specific, quantified guidance for your vehicle, your driving pattern, and your current engine condition.

### What You Get

**Better MPG — starting immediately.** Typical improvement: 3–12% for most drivers. Vehicles with deferred maintenance: 15% or more.

**Real-time guidance.** The app reads your engine 10 times per second and delivers coaching in real time — when to ease off the throttle, when to coast, optimal shift points for your specific vehicle's torque curve.

**Maintenance predictions.** Lume-Auto watches your spark plugs, fuel injectors, air filter, oxygen sensors, catalytic converter, and tires degrade in real time. It tells you what's starting to go wrong before it fails — and tells you exactly how much MPG you're losing because of it.

**Fuel quality alerts.** The system detects poor fuel quality from your engine's behavior and alerts you.

**A running efficiency score.** Your vehicle's current MPG as a percentage of what it's capable of. Watch it improve over time.

### How It Works

Lume-Auto treats your engine like what it actually is: a deterministic flow system. Air flows in, fuel flows in, combustion happens, torque flows out, exhaust flows out. Every part of that sequence is measurable and governable.

The organism builds a model of your specific engine — its individual fuel map, its combustion characteristics, its current component health — over your first few hundred miles. After that, it knows your car better than any generic app could.

When something drifts from optimal — your throttle behavior, your engine's timing, a component wearing down — the organism detects it, quantifies it in MPG terms, and tells you what to do.

Everything is deterministic. Same car, same conditions, same output. No AI guessing. No hallucination. Verified, reproducible, auditable efficiency governance.

## FAQ SECTION

**Will this void my warranty?**  
No. Lume-Auto reads data from your OBD-II port. It does not write to your ECU, modify any software, or change any settings. OBD-II reading is a standard diagnostic function that every mechanic uses.

**Does it work on my car?**  
If your vehicle was sold after January 1, 1996 in the United States (or 2001 in Europe), it has an OBD-II port and Lume-Auto is compatible. This covers virtually every car on the road today.

**How quickly will I see results?**  
Behavioral guidance begins immediately. The organism builds your vehicle-specific model over 200–500 miles. Full efficiency optimization is active after that.

**Is it safe?**  
Yes. Lume-Auto has three hard constraints it can never violate: it will never suggest anything that could damage your engine, never suggest anything that affects your emissions systems, and never provide guidance during active safety events (emergency braking, stability control, etc.).

**Do I need to be connected to the internet?**  
No. Core governance runs entirely on-device. Internet connection enables fleet aggregation and model refinement but is not required for daily operation.

**Will it work on a hybrid?**  
Yes for the ICE portion of any hybrid. Full hybrid energy flow governance is a future feature.

**What OBD-II adapter do I need?**  
Any ELM327-compatible Bluetooth adapter works. We recommend the adapter included with Lume-Auto. Existing adapters from other apps are compatible.
