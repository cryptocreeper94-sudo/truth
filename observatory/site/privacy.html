PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. DATA WE COLLECT

When you create an account, we collect your email address and payment information (processed by Stripe — we do not store card numbers). We collect basic usage analytics (page views, feature usage) to improve the Service.

2. DATA WE DO NOT COLLECT

We do not collect location data, device fingerprints, advertising identifiers, or sell any user data to third parties. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA

  • Email: account management, subscription receipts, service announcements.
  • Payment data: processed securely by Stripe, Inc. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Usage data: aggregate analytics to improve the Service. Never shared externally.

4. SMS COMMUNICATIONS

If you opt in to SMS alerts, we use Twilio to deliver atmospheric event notifications to your phone number. You may opt out at any time by replying STOP or visiting your account settings. Message frequency varies. Standard message and data rates apply.

5. DATA RETENTION

Account data is retained for the duration of your subscription. Upon account deletion, your personal data is purged within 30 days. Anonymized usage analytics may be retained indefinitely.

6. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Twilio — SMS delivery (if opted in)
  • Hetzner — server infrastructure (Germany/Finland)

7. YOUR RIGHTS

You may request a copy of your data, request deletion, or update your information at any time by contacting us.
