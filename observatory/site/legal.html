DATA SOURCES & ATTRIBUTION

Truth Observatory ingests and displays data from the following public sources. All data is presented unmodified with source attribution and retrieval timestamps.

  • NOAA / NWS — NEXRAD radar (via Iowa State Mesonet), GOES satellite imagery, Global Lightning Mapper (GLM), Space Weather Prediction Center (SWPC), geomagnetic indices
  • USGS — Earthquake Hazards Program seismic feed
  • FAA — NOTAMs (Notices to Air Missions)
  • EPA — Air quality monitoring data
  • EIA — Electrical grid demand (EIA-930)
  • Iowa State Mesonet — Surface weather observations (ASOS/AWOS), satellite tile cache
  • RainViewer — Global precipitation radar composite
  • ADS-B Exchange / OpenSky — Aircraft transponder data
  • OpenCelliD — Cell tower infrastructure data
