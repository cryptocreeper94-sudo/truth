TEXT ALERTS (SMS)COMING SOON

Text alerts are coming soon. Sign-up opens once carrier registration is approved. Email alerts work today: turn them on in your cockpit.

Truth Observatory can text you when something you chose happens. Texts are for paying subscribers only and use the same alert types and ZIP code as your email alerts. Nothing is sent unless an event matches.

WHAT YOU CAN RECEIVE

  • National Weather Service warnings for your ZIP code, at the severity you pick (moderate, severe or extreme)
  • Earthquakes worldwide at or above the magnitude you pick (M5.0 to M7.0)
  • Geomagnetic storms at NOAA level G3 or higher
  • US volcanoes raised to WATCH or WARNING by the USGS

The daily brief is email only. Texts never contain ads or promotions.
