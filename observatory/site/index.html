Operating Principle

The Instrument, Not the Interpreter

The Truth Observatory collects raw atmospheric, geophysical, and infrastructure data from public scientific sources. Every observation is hashed at collection time, logged to a permanent manifest, and stored with full provenance metadata. The raw artifact is the evidence. The manifest is the chain of custody.

We do not editorialize. We do not filter for narrative fit. When streams coincide — a geomagnetic anomaly alongside an unusual radar pattern alongside a grid demand spike — the system flags the temporal proximity. Proximity is not causation. That distinction is enforced architecturally, not by policy.

"The system must be capable of disproving the investigator's preferred explanation. If the skeptic engine is disabled, it becomes advocacy software."
